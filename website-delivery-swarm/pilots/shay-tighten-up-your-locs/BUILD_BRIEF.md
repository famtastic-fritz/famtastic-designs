# Tighten Up Your Locs — interactive proof brief

Build a polished, responsive, static HTML/CSS/JavaScript prototype from `visual-directions/omg-v1.png`.

## Visual contract

- Preserve the cinematic obsidian, copper, pearl, and magenta art direction.
- Make long loc strands feel like part of the page architecture through CSS curves, lines, masks, or layered imagery.
- Preserve the strong editorial typography and spacious premium composition.
- Use the approved mockup itself only as a visual reference or clearly labeled concept panel; do not pretend it is real client photography.
- The page must look intentionally designed at desktop and mobile sizes.

## Experience contract

- Hero copy: `YOUR CROWN. YOUR JOURNEY. YOUR LEGACY.`
- Primary action: `Begin Your Loc Journey`.
- Include an accessible before/crowned transformation control.
- Include services for consultation, installation, retightening, and loc care without inventing prices.
- Include an interactive consultation form that demonstrates validation and a local success state but sends no data.
- Include a founder section for Shay. Do not invent biography, credentials, address, phone number, email address, reviews, prices, availability, or certification.
- Mark all unverified business information as awaiting owner confirmation.
- Include a visible prototype disclosure.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, keyboard operation, focus states, contrast, and semantic headings.

## Files

Create `site/index.html`, `site/styles.css`, and `site/script.js`. Keep everything dependency-free and runnable from a static file server.
