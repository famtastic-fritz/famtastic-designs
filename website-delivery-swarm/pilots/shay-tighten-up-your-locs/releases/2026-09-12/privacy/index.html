Privacy Policy

Last updated September 14, 2026

This policy explains how Tighten Up Your Locs handles information through this website. For privacy questions, contact Shay at hello@tightenupyourlocs.com.

Information you choose to send

When you submit an appointment request or question, the form sends your name, email address, service selection and preferred time, along with any phone number or message you provide. These details are used to review your request, contact you and manage your appointment. Visiting the site alone does not submit this form. Please do not include payment details or sensitive medical information.

Request handling and service providers

Requests and appointment records are handled in Tighten Up Your Locs’ own booking system and database. Shay manages them through this website’s private admin. Hosting and email providers support storage and appointment-related communication. Submitting a request does not enroll you in promotional messages. Request references, appointment status and related communications help us respond and manage scheduling.

The private admin uses necessary session cookies for secure sign-in. These are separate from public website measurement; private admin pages do not load Analytics.

The Locs Letter

The newsletter is optional and separate from booking. If you choose to sign up, we use your email address to send a confirmation request. You must confirm before receiving The Locs Letter, which may include loc-care notes, opening announcements and business updates. Subscription and consent records are stored in Tighten Up Your Locs’ own system; email providers help deliver the messages. You can unsubscribe using the link in a newsletter or contact Shay for help. Newsletter form contents are not sent to Analytics.

Website measurement and technical data

The homepage uses Google Analytics to measure page views and request actions. Analytics and advertising cookie storage are disabled; basic measurements may still be sent to Google without those cookies. The implementation does not send form contents to Analytics and removes page query strings, fragments and referrer values from its configured event parameters. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled.

Previously saved tracking refusals, browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals prevent the Analytics tag from loading. Existing Analytics cookies from the earlier implementation are removed when the updated homepage loads. Browser extensions may also block measurement.

Hosting and security systems process technical information such as IP addresses to deliver the website and limit abusive requests. The site also requests fonts from Google Fonts. These third-party services receive connection information needed to provide their services; see Google’s Privacy Policy.

Stored requests and your choices

Request and appointment records are stored for scheduling, follow-up and business recordkeeping; they are not automatically erased when you close the page. Contact Shay to ask about stored information or request a correction or deletion. Handling a request may require verifying that it relates to you and considering records that must be retained for business or legal obligations. No fixed deletion deadline is promised by this website.

Updates and contact

Changes to this policy will appear on this page with an updated date. For questions or assistance, email hello@tightenupyourlocs.com.
