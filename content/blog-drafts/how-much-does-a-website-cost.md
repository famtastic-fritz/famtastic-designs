Ask three web designers what a website costs and you'll get three answers between $500 and $15,000. That's not because anyone's lying to you. It's because "a website" describes everything from a single page with your phone number on it to a system that takes bookings, processes payments, and syncs to your accounting software.

So here are real numbers, and what separates them.

## The short answer

That number surprises people, so it's worth explaining rather than just asserting.

## Why quotes vary so much

Three things drive the price, and most quotes don't separate them.

**How much is custom.** A site designed from a blank page costs more than one built on a proven structure and fitted to your business. Both can look good. Only one requires someone to invent a layout from scratch, which is where the hours go.

**How many moving parts.** A site that shows your services and takes enquiries is one thing. A site with online ordering, member logins, inventory, or booking that syncs to your calendar is a different job with a different price. Every feature is something that has to be built, tested, and then maintained when it breaks.

**How much of the work is yours.** If you hand over your logo, your service list, and your photos, the build is fast. If the designer has to write your copy, source images, and chase you for details for three weeks, that time gets billed.

Most inflated quotes come from bundling all three together and pricing for the worst case.

Two days for the Quick Start, once we have your content. Larger builds run four to six weeks.

The bottleneck is almost never the building. It's waiting on your logo, your photos, and a decision about what your services are actually called. Businesses that have that ready get live fast. If you don't have it ready, that's fine — we'll help you shape it, it just adds time.

## What to ask before you pay anyone

Whoever you hire, get these in writing:

- **What exactly is included**, listed as items, not adjectives.
- **What is excluded** and what it would cost to add.
- **Whether there's a recurring charge**, and what happens if you stop paying it.
- **Who owns the domain.** It should be in your name. Always.
- **How many revisions** are included before changes cost extra.

A designer who can answer all five quickly is a designer who has done this before. One who gets vague around the fourth question is telling you something.
