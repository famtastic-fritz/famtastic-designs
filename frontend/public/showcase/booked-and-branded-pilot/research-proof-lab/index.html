  7. type-01Pair oversized compressed display text with tabular service facts and bounded paragraph widths; expressive type owns headlines, not long reading.research-informed
  8. type-02Use editorial serif contrast for emotional phrases and sans-serif for actions, facts, and form labels so the reading task stays clear.research-informed
  9. type-03Give both languages the same typographic status; language order is an owner-controlled field rather than an ethnicity-derived template assumption.content-integrity-rule
