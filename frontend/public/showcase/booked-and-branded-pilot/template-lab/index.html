The $199 foundation · proposed Booked & Branded scope

Booking is the value add.
The website basics do not disappear.

The starter must already feel like a serious business website. The booking path, payment QR, and phone workflow make it more useful; they do not replace identity, contact, location, services, content, or trust.

01

Your web address

A custom domain or an owner-controlled existing domain connected to the new site.

02

One branded forwarding address

A customer-approved address such as bookings@yourdomain.com delivers into the inbox the owner already checks. A hosted mailbox and sending as that address remain an upgrade.

03

Contact that works

A protected contact form with verified delivery, plus click-to-call, click-to-text, and approved social links.

04

Find the business

Location or service area, accurate hours, parking or arrival notes, and a map only when the owner wants one.

05

Services before DMs

Services, starting prices, duration, preparation, policies, and what the client should choose next.

06

Proof of the work

A responsive, owner-approved gallery and consent-based testimonials—not copied reviews or invented results.

07

Booking that fits today

Keep Booksy or another current provider, connect Google or Cal.com, or use FAMtastic request-to-book. No forced overnight switch.

08

Owner-controlled QR

Display the business’s approved Cash App or existing payment QR. FAMtastic does not process or receive the payment.

09

Launch quality

One year of hosting, SSL, responsive behavior, keyboard access, reduced-motion support, performance checks, and launch QA.

Meet owners where they are

Do not demand a breakup before the first date.

The branded site can send clients into the owner’s current Booksy or other provider account while the owner keeps the calendar and client workflow they know. The website immediately improves the story around that booking link.
