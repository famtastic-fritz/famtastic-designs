What the pilot contains

A brand layer and a daily-work layer.

The public website explains the business with its own visual language. The phone Booking Desk gives one owner a bounded place to see requests, make decisions, manage services, and hand clients to a business-owned payment destination.

  • One custom mobile-first website direction selected from three working proofs
  • One-owner, one-location phone Booking Desk starter
  • Up to 12 services with duration, price, preparation, and policy notes
  • Request-to-book with confirm, propose-another-time, or decline decisions
  • Blackout dates, availability windows, and request status
  • Business-owned Square, Stripe Payment Link, Cash App Business, or reviewed payment handoff
  • A decorative-to-live QR placement after the business connects its own approved destination
  • Fresh consent-based testimonials with owner moderation
  • One year of FAMtastic-managed hosting and the existing Web Basics domain path
  • Shay-guided setup, proof explanation, and launch handoff

Phone-first by design

Small enough to learn. Useful enough to matter.

The starter is intentionally not a fake all-in-one platform. It centers the decisions a solo barber or stylist actually makes between appointments.
