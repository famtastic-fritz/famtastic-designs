# Scope

**Surface:** authenticated customer `/portal?section=projects`, with an exact
Tighten Up Your Locs proof-review link on a 390px phone.

**Primary user:** a nontechnical small-business owner who has completed the
brief and needs to understand one thing: whether to choose a design, answer a
question, or wait for FAMtastic.

**Primary task:** open a project, understand what is happening, review three
current directions only when they are selectable, choose one, or request a
replacement round without getting lost in infrastructure or operator detail.

**Constraints:** durable Drupal records only; no invented readiness; 44px
targets; one-glow rule; account-only proof access; research explains decisions
but never promises growth.
