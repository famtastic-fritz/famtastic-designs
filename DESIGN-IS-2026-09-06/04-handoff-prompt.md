# Handoff prompt

Build a mobile-first FAMtastic customer project experience from `design.md`.
Start with a list of projects. After a project opens, present only the customer
stage and its one next action. Use four progressive destinations: Today,
Concepts, Research & growth plan, and Setup; put files, source artifacts, and
archive under More. Never render a selectable proof control after a revision
request. Keep technical infrastructure and Build DNA out of the customer
critical path. Use branded, versioned FAMtastic Concierge transactional emails
with a named graphical CTA, no visible opaque portal URL. Research must explain
the creative choice in plain language and attach a labeled 30/60/90-day
opportunity plan without promising results.
