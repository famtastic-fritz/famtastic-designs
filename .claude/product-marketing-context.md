# FAMtastic Designs product-marketing context

This file is shared context for Claude, Codex, Shay, and installed marketing skills. Repository contracts override generic marketing guidance.

## Position

FAMtastic Designs engineers connected digital business systems. A website may be the visible entry point, but the useful system can include discovery, lead response, Commerce, onboarding, customer service, analytics, automation, portals, AI assistance, and retention.

## Primary audience

Small and growing businesses that need a practical digital starting point or have a website that is not helping customers take the next step. Speak to the business problem in plain language before discussing architecture.

## Core customer jobs

1. Get Found
2. Get Customers
3. Get Paid
4. Serve Customers
5. Grow and Automate

## Differentiation

- FAMtastic proves capabilities in its own operating system before selling them.
- Drupal provides structured content, Commerce, identity, workflow, and staff operations; React provides the branded customer experience.
- Offers begin with the smallest useful system and can grow through evidence-based additions.
- The work connects acquisition through service and retention instead of stopping at page delivery.

## Proof discipline

Use `docs/CAPABILITY_REGISTRY.md` for claim status and evidence. Never invent revenue results, testimonials, statistics, scarcity, or customer outcomes. Distinguish implemented, locally proven, provider-proven, production-smoke-tested, approval-gated, and planned behavior.

## Voice

Clear, direct, confident, practical, and human. Teach without condescension. Explain what a capability changes for the customer. Avoid empty futurism, jargon piles, and claims that every business needs every feature.

## Commercial boundaries

The $199 Web Basics Bundle is one bounded entry offer, not the automatic answer to every website request. Recommendations follow intake. Generated prices, discounts, recurring terms, final legal promises, promotional sends, advertising spend, and broad publication remain approval-gated.

## Content and CTA model

Create pillar-and-spoke series that answer related buyer questions. Every article has one intent, a controlled category and tags, capability evidence, related FAQs, internal links, and one relevant next action. Prefer an assessment, recommendation, proof, configured purchase, support path, or scoped conversation over a generic “contact us.”

Canonical doctrine and content live in `docs/DEMAND_ENGINE_DOCTRINE.md` and `backend/config/famtastic-content-series.json`.
