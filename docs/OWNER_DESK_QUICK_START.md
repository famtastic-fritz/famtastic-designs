# Tighten Up Your Locs — Owner Desk quick start

Owner entry: https://famtasticdesigns.com/portal?section=booking

The business website remains https://tightenupyourlocs.com/. The private Owner Desk lives in the FAMtastic Designs portal, not a public `/owner` page on the business site.

1. Sign in using the existing verified Shay account and its existing password. If needed, select **Forgot password?** on the login screen. Do not create a second account. No new password is issued with this release.
2. In Owner Desk, choose **America/New_York** under **Calendar timezone** before entering times. The initial zone can come from the device; check it especially when traveling.
3. Open **Requests** to review customer inquiries. A request alone does not reserve time. Expand **Confirm or propose a time**, enter the start and end, then use **Confirm appointment** when that time is agreed, or propose an alternative for the customer to accept through a private email link.
4. Use **Calendar** and its day selector to see saved appointments. **Propose a new time** starts a reschedule proposal; the original confirmed time stays reserved until the replacement is accepted. Use **Cancel appointment** when necessary and **Mark complete** after the visit.
5. In **Openings**, enter a label and start/end. Choose whether to publish it, then **Save opening**. Draft openings remain private; published openings can appear on the customer site. Openings are invitations to request, not automatic confirmed appointments.
6. Refresh after a connection issue before assuming an action failed. A saved confirmation and a queued email are different states; “Notification queued” does not prove delivery to the customer's inbox.

Bookings are stored in the backend, not just this device. Access remains tied to the verified business-owner account. Fritz's separate customer account is not automatically granted access to Shay's private appointment records; this release does not change roles or sharing.

Not included in this release: Google/Apple/Booksy calendar synchronization, payment/deposit collection, SMS reminders, classes, course content or LMS. Continue checking any separate calendar until a real synchronization integration is added.

If the desk says no website is linked, confirm that the signed-in account is Shay's existing account. For account help use support@famtasticdesigns.com. Never send a password by email.
