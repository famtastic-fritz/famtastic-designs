# Shay customer communication voice — scoped research follow-up

## Goal

Create a durable Shay communication system that feels lively, capable, warm, and unmistakably FAMtastic while remaining truthful about delivery state, payment, and customer action.

This is a research and approval task. It does not authorize an email, text message, campaign, account mutation, or customer-state transition.

## Evidence to collect

- Owner-approved Shay-signed messages that reached the intended recipient.
- Fritz's corrections to customer-facing tone, sequence, and calls to action.
- The active FAMtastic email design system and versioned transactional templates.
- Customer replies that show whether a message was clear, welcoming, and actionable.
- Portal language that customers successfully used without a separate text explanation.

Private raw messages, addresses, headers, bearer links, and provider identifiers stay outside Git. The research ledger retains sanitized patterns and references to protected evidence.

## Deliverables

1. A short Shay voice charter covering warmth, wit, confidence, clarity, and service boundaries.
2. A do-and-don't table for greetings, progress updates, requests, payment language, blockers, and handoff messages.
3. Approved examples for subject lines, openings, proof explanations, one-action calls to action, and Shay's sign-off.
4. A standard/v2 visual proof at phone and desktop widths using non-customer fixture data.
5. A review checklist that verifies claims, links, sender identity, recipient scope, template version, accessible branding, and send authorization.

## Acceptance boundary

Fritz approves the charter and examples before they become a reusable sending standard. One approved The Reckoning email may be evidence for the study, but a single message does not establish a universal Shay voice. The resulting system must keep preview, queued, provider-accepted, inbox-received, and customer-read states separate.
