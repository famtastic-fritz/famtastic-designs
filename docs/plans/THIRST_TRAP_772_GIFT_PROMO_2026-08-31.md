# Thirst Trap 772 gift promo

## Goal

Create a shareable, mobile-first gift concept for the real Vero Beach pop-up
drink brand shown in the owner-supplied photographs. The page should make the
pink tent memorable, connect people to the verified public social accounts,
and give event organizers a clear next step without inventing menu items,
prices, testimonials, availability, or contact details.

## Confirmed public signals

- Brand: Thirst Trap / Thirst Trap 772.
- Line visible in the owner-supplied booth: `CRAVE · DRINK · REPEAT`.
- Public position: lemonade, frozen drinks, tropical fruit, desserts, snacks,
  and event booking, as described by the current Facebook search surface.
- Instagram: `https://www.instagram.com/thirst_trap772/`.
- Facebook: `https://www.facebook.com/ThirstTrap772/`.
- Market: Vero Beach / Treasure Coast, based on the Facebook page title and
  the 772 brand identity.

## Truth and privacy boundary

- The owner-supplied photographs are visual references, not consent to expose
  every person or contact detail visible inside them.
- Photographs showing a child or conflicting email details are not published.
- The user reported a Yahoo business email, while a supplied booth photograph
  appears to show a different provider. No email address is published until
  the exact address is confirmed by the business.
- No prices, flavors, schedule, inventory, payment, order, delivery, or event
  availability are represented as active.
- No social account is followed, messaged, posted to, or modified.

## Page recipe

1. `thirst-nav-01` — small mobile-first brand and social actions.
2. `thirst-hero-01` — bold brand world with the pink tent and cold-pouch visual.
3. `thirst-menu-01` — category-level “what may be pouring” cards without
   fabricated flavors or prices.
4. `thirst-event-01` — event-booking position and direct social handoff.
5. `thirst-origin-01` — owner-supplied market energy translated into an owned
   digital front door.
6. `thirst-social-01` — reusable native-text promo cards plus verified links.
7. `thirst-gift-01` — transparent FAMtastic gift note and approval boundary.

## Release boundary

The build may be published as an unlisted, no-index gift concept after local
acceptance and governed frontend deployment. It does not become the business's
official website, accept orders, publish social content, or disclose an email.
