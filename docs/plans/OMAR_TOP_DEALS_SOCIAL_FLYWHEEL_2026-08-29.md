# Omar Top Deals social flywheel

Date: 2026-08-29  
Status: working showcase refinement; no social account, post, schedule, message, event, or campaign activated

## Goal

Turn Omar's current handbill strength into a durable, owner-controlled marketing loop without pretending print is obsolete. The flyer earns the first look. The approved site and QR preserve the relationship after the paper changes hands.

## Transformation

1. Omar confirms one permanent public link and one QR.
2. The current flyer, table sign, bag insert, and social bio point to that same front door.
3. Omar changes the next confirmed pop-up and featured categories without replacing the link.
4. Three repeatable social formats send attention back to the front door:
   - Fresh Finds: category energy without unconfirmed inventory promises.
   - Where Omar Pops Up: confirmed location, time, directions, and day-of context.
   - The Return Loop: save the link, ask about a find, or explicitly choose future updates.
5. The owner desk retains caption drafts and content pillars. Copying a draft is local utility, not publishing authority.

## Creative bridge

- Current-state proof: owner-supplied Just Africa flyer, publicly shown only as a crop with its contact strip removed.
- New premium family: three separate reference-led, text-free marketing scenes for feed, Story/Reel, and follow-up use.
- Readable headlines, labels, disclosures, and CTAs stay native HTML/CSS.
- The print palette becomes a modern system: sunset orange, gold, deep ink, paper grain, tactile edges, diagonal handbill rhythm, and editorial photography.

## Offer logic

The useful first step remains Omar's $199 Web Basics front door: approved one-page site, custom-domain setup path, forwarding business email, featured categories, next pop-up, contact/directions, and verified QR. Durable customer records, protected login, social publishing, scheduling, analytics, messaging, and automation are later scoped additions; none are represented as included or active here.

## Approval gates

Omar must approve the brand name, handle, profile ownership, public photographs, product/category language, event facts, contact route, payment route, caption copy, publishing schedule, customer-permission policy, and any real launch. FAMtastic must not invent inventory, availability, results, engagement, urgency, or an active social presence.

- The owner console exposes three caption drafts and four content pillars.
