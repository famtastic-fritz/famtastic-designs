# Gemini Flash Lite story-build result — 2026-08-20

## What was proven

The **Lite Image Story** turned one premium art-direction reference into a
cohesive five-image visual story and responsive static page:

1. visual source/canon — an inherited OpenArt `gpt-image-2` campaign image;
2. one Gemini `gemini-3.1-flash-lite-image` reference-assisted hero recreation;
3. four Gemini Flash Lite supporting images: ritual, recognition, return, and
   inheritance;
4. an original static editorial page using local Metropolis/Lora fonts,
   non-trademark stadium-rhythm lattice, ticket/paper texture, grain, depth,
   responsive art direction, and accessible reduced-motion behavior;
5. browser proof at 1440px and 390px.

This is **locally proven creative/evidence work**, not a proof of a provider
invoice, independent visual approval, customer delivery, Site Studio execution,
or production publication.

## Why Lite matched the premium visual direction

The quality jump did not come from writing “make it premium.” It came from a
more complete visual contract before generation:

- a literal transcription of the premium reference's camera angle, subject
  layout, architecture, light, palette, material, and negative-space logic;
- a clear statement that the reference was art direction only—not pixels, faces,
  composition, or affiliated documentation to copy;
- one story purpose per image, with unique setting, cast, action, camera,
  aspect ratio, texture, and typography-safe negative space;
- tactile materials (wet concrete, leather, denim, glass, wool, wood, paper),
  believable candid emotion, correct anatomy, and explicit exclusions for
  text, marks, cloned people, fake signage, and unwanted imagery;
- deterministic page assembly that gives each chapter a different surface and
  type composition instead of recoloring a repeated card.

The exact prompts, generation responses, usage metadata, timings, expected
costs, and asset hashes are in the run's Build DNA and receipts.
