# What will your website cost after the first year?

FAMtastic Designs includes a standard domain and the first 12 months of hosting with the $199 website special and $119 landing page special. After the included year, you choose $9.99 per month or $100 for the next year.

## Website pricing should include the costs around the build

A low build price can become expensive when the invoice leaves out the domain, hosting, security certificate, or renewal terms. Business owners deserve the full picture before work starts.

The FAMtastic specials separate the build from future hosting while giving you the first year to launch, learn, and grow.

## What the first year includes

Both specials include:

- **Website or landing page build**: based on the selected offer
- **Standard domain registration**: one available non-premium domain for the first year
- **Hosting**: 12 months beginning at launch
- **Secure connection**: Secure Sockets Layer setup for supported domains
- **Ownership clarity**: you own or control the domain

Premium domains, aftermarket purchases, paid email, advertising, and advanced third-party services cost extra when needed.

## What happens when the included year ends

Before month 13, choose one hosting option:

| Renewal option | Price | Effective cost |
|---|---:|---:|
| Monthly hosting | $9.99 per month | $119.88 across 12 months |
| Annual hosting | $100 for the next year | About $8.33 per month |

The annual option saves $19.88 compared with 12 monthly payments.

Renewal requires separate authorization. FAMtastic does not convert the original purchase into a recurring charge without your approval.

## Your domain should never become a lock-in tool

The domain represents your business. You should know who controls it, where it is registered, and how to transfer it.

FAMtastic can connect or manage the domain as part of the service, but ownership stays with you. That keeps the relationship focused on results instead of access control.
