# FAMtastic Designs special-offer blog series

This campaign promotes the full FAMtastic Designs service ladder while giving readers a concrete entry point: a $199 website special and a $119 landing page special.

## Campaign plan

- **Goal**: turn local businesses, startups, side hustles, and solo professionals into qualified project inquiries
- **Primary offer**: one-page website for $199
- **Secondary offer**: conversion-focused landing page for $119
- **Included with each special**: standard domain registration for the first year, subject to availability, plus 12 months of hosting
- **Renewal after the included year**: customer chooses $9.99 per month or $100 for the next year, with separate authorization
- **Primary call to action**: start at `https://famtasticdesigns.com/contact`
- **Brand promise**: engineered for the business, mobile-first, lead-focused, AI-ready, and never built from a cookie-cutter template

## Offer guardrails

- The 48-hour delivery window begins after FAMtastic receives the completed intake, content, assets, and approval to start
- Domain registration is limited to a standard available domain; premium and aftermarket domains are excluded
- The customer owns or controls the domain
- Hosting renewal starts only after the included 12 months and requires separate authorization
- Third-party fees, paid advertising, advanced integrations, e-commerce, and custom application work require separate scope
- Every post should link to the matching public offer once the live pricing pages reflect the campaign

## Image direction

Each post uses a wide, text-free editorial header so the same image can work on the blog, email, and social platforms. The visual system follows the live site: near-black surfaces, electric lime accents, geometric engineering lines, crisp interface forms, and confident negative space.
