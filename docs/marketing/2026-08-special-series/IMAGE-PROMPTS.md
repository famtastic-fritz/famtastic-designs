# Image generation prompts

These five text-free blog headers were generated with the built-in image generation tool. The existing FAMtastic Designs Open Graph image served only as a palette and visual-style reference.

## Shared direction

- **Use case**: ads-marketing
- **Asset type**: 16:9 wide blog header
- **Style**: sophisticated 3D editorial illustration with crisp enterprise interface geometry
- **Backdrop**: near-black engineering environment with restrained grids and technical drafting lines
- **Palette**: near-black, charcoal, white, and electric lime
- **Layout**: subject concentrated toward the center-right with negative space for an editorial headline
- **Constraints**: no words, numbers, currency symbols, logos, trademarks, watermarks, or pseudo-text

## 01: The $199 website special

Create a premium editorial campaign visual representing a professional one-page business website launching for a small business. Show one polished mobile-first website interface floating above a clean device frame, with a contact form and a small incoming lead notification suggested through abstract interface shapes. Keep the mood confident, controlled, and premium rather than flashy.

## 02: The $119 landing page special

Create a premium editorial campaign visual showing a focused conversion journey from a digital ad into one streamlined landing page and one successful lead signal. Show a small abstract ad card flowing through one luminous path into a single polished mobile landing-page interface, ending in one bright confirmation pulse. Keep the journey focused and free from competing calls to action.

## 03: Domain and hosting without surprises

Create a premium editorial visual linking a customer-owned domain, secure web hosting, and a clear one-year renewal timeline. Render three connected premium objects: an abstract globe or domain node, a secure hosting server stack with a shield, and a twelve-segment circular timeline. Suggest customer control and pricing clarity without lock-in imagery.

## 04: Built for leads, not likes

Create a premium editorial campaign visual of a mobile-first business website transforming anonymous visitor signals into a clean, organized lead pipeline. Show a polished smartphone interface feeding a short sequence of abstract visitor dots into three lead cards and one bright contact notification. Emphasize measurable action rather than social vanity.

## 05: Start small and build the right system

Create a premium editorial visual of one strong website core expanding into four connected business capabilities: artificial intelligence conversation, client portal, online store, and analytics automation. Represent each module with clear text-free icons and interface forms. Make the modules feel connected to one system rather than unrelated applications.
