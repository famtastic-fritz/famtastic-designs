# What should your business build after the first website?

Your first website should solve the immediate problem. Your next system should remove the bottleneck that growth exposes. FAMtastic Designs can start with a $199 website or $119 landing page, then expand the digital stack when the business needs more.

A new business may need one trusted page, one clear offer, and one contact form. An active ad campaign may need a dedicated landing page that matches the promotion.

## Add the next system when the workflow demands it

FAMtastic Designs supports a wider service ladder:

- **Site rebuild and redesign**: modernize an outdated site, improve mobile performance, and repair the lead path
- **Custom website development**: build multiple pages, custom features, analytics, and structured content around the business
- **Artificial intelligence chatbot**: answer common questions, qualify leads, and support appointment requests around the clock
- **Client portal**: give customers a branded place for project updates, files, messages, and payments
- **E-commerce**: sell products through a secure store with catalog, inventory, shipping, and payment workflows
- **Automation and intelligent applications**: connect forms, customer data, operations, and follow-up into one working system

## The roadmap should follow the business problem

More features do not automatically create more value. The best next build targets a measurable bottleneck.

Examples include:

- Missed calls after hours suggest a chatbot or automated intake
- Repeated status emails suggest a client portal
- Manual lead handoffs suggest customer relationship management integration
- Strong ad traffic with weak conversion suggests a focused landing page
- An outdated mobile experience suggests a site rebuild

FAMtastic begins with discovery, confirms a fixed scope, and verifies the system before launch.

Start with the entry point that matches today’s goal. Use the $199 website to establish the business online. Use the $119 landing page to support one campaign. Expand when a real workflow or revenue opportunity calls for it.
