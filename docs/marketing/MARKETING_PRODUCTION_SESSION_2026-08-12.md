provider proof. The reusable result is not merely a collection of advertisements;
it is a governed path from offer truth to content, media, approval, publishing,
verification, attribution, and future productization.

- Remotion production and campaign command center: locally proven.

- Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and X: planned and asset-compatible, but

- No claim of campaign revenue, customer acquisition, or return on investment

## Commercial reuse

The session supports future offers for campaign foundations, branded content
sprints, short-form video packs, social publishing operations, marketing command
centers, and complete campaign-automation implementations. These require a
repeatable onboarding contract covering scope, channel identities, asset counts,
approvals, provider costs, revision limits, posting responsibility, measurement,
support, and acceptance evidence.

## Content reuse

The lessons should become the **From One Offer to a Measurable Campaign** blog
and tutorial series defined in `docs/CAPABILITY_TO_REVENUE_STRATEGY.md`. Each
piece must use real implementation evidence, avoid invented results, and point
to a relevant assessment, demonstration, or scoped service—not a generic CTA.
