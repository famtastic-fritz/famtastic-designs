The actual local 1,000-row workbook contributes 100 broad-category candidate rows for each initial niche. The 260-row workbook is historical contact evidence and contributes no new automatically eligible recipient. Their email values do not overlap; that does not prove that the 1,000 were never contacted through another route.

Current result: **zero verified eligible recipients; shortfall 50 beauty, 50 detailing, 50 baking**. This is a held cohort, not an invented list of 150. The aggregate in `cohort-aggregate.json` records exact workbook hashes and counts without private rows. All 300 target-niche email addresses use consumer-mail domains; syntax and domain availability cannot establish business ownership or recipient deliverability.

A bounded review inspected the first four actual records in each niche: 12 candidates. Exact business names plus the supplied city did not surface a verified matching official owner website/contact identity. One official near-match for a baking name showed GBP pricing and Southam collection, which did not establish the listed Tampa business ([merchant page](https://epicbakes.com/products/giant-brownie)). Unrelated search results were rejected. This finding is limited to this bounded review; it does not establish that the businesses are fake, that no websites exist, or that all 300 were individually researched. The remaining 288 candidates are unreviewed. Actual private review details and contact fields are in ignored `.data/acquisition-199/bounded-review.json` with mode 0600.

The precise gaps are: official business/source identity; exact contact ownership; jurisdiction and lawful/provider eligibility; address verification; actual existing-site and booking facts; native prior-contact/reply/purchase/suppression reconciliation. A supplied location is not jurisdiction proof. No receipt substitutes a missing source with a guess. No email delivery probe or paid verifier was used. Historical 260 rows require reviewed re-entry, never a default restart.

Keep row-bound receipts in `.data/acquisition-199/`; provide their JSON through `--evidence`. Each verified gate needs current check/expiry time, reviewer, source/artifact hash, and the exact candidate key/contact. Site/booking absence must be explicitly verified. History requires a native ledger revision and explicit no suppression, human reply or purchase, plus reviewed prior-contact state. Unsupported or duplicate contacts/businesses remain held.

Every gate also requires an `artifact_path` relative to the private cohort directory. The tool resolves the path, rejects traversal or escaped symlinks, verifies that the actual file exists and compares its SHA-256 with the receipt. Integrity and current reviewer attestation do not automatically certify real-world factual truth: the reviewer must actually establish the business/source/contact/legal/provider facts. Source HTML, address-verification receipt and native history export remain private. A guessed 64-character string cannot qualify a record.

Accepted private cohort rows use canonical niche IDs `beauty_hair`, `mobile_detailing`, `baking_catering`. Their staff-only `qualification` bridge maps business+source → business, contact ownership+address verification → contact, jurisdiction → jurisdiction, provider eligibility → provider, reconciled history → history, and verified site/booking facts → bindings. It supplies `receipt=cohort:<canonical receipt hash>` and `sha256=<canonical receipt hash>` for `AcquisitionSampleService::issue()`. The complete row-bound receipt and checked artifact files stay private; the opaque hash binds the server handoff. Qualification is not exact sending approval, formal customer proof selection or consent to a charge.

Only fully eligible businesses can enter the first 50 per niche. Randomization assigns generic/tailored arms once within niche at business level, then persists and reuses that assignment. Cohort changes require archiving the previous private assignment rather than silently rerandomizing it. Price, sender and timing remain fixed. Primary measures are paid-customer counts and measured contribution; 150 businesses provide operational discovery, not a statistically established winning arm. Synthetic accepted-gate/expiry/unsafe-link/suppression/reply/history/randomization fixtures run with `python3 scripts/acquisition-cohort-test.py` and do not qualify real prospects.

Scope guard: the original category “Mobile Detailing, Auto Care & Tinting” includes activities outside this mobile-detailing campaign. Broad category membership cannot qualify mechanics or tint-only businesses. Every receipt must include top-level niche_confirmed=true and confirmed_niche matching the row’s canonical approved niche, backed by a verified niche gate with current source URL/reviewer/time and a hash-checked private source artifact. The same rule prevents treating a broad beauty or baking/private-chef category as proof of the approved business activity. An activity beyond the approved beauty/hair, mobile-detailing or baking/catering scope remains held until separately approved; changing a category label is insufficient. The server handoff carries these confirmed fields and receipt hash. The synthetic valid fixture explicitly confirms mobile_detailing; missing confirmation, unverified niche source, mechanics, tinting, private-chef-only and product-only mismatches are rejected.
