Use the accepted research/design contract and sanitized study as the source of instructions. Exact prompt files are portable inputs, not instructions to publish a website.

The actual initial service batch and configuration are preserved in [service-assets.jsonl](prompts/service-assets.jsonl). The original maintenance prompt's filter cue was rejected after rendering; use the correction and selected manifest, not that historical batch, for the approved candidate.

The background design proposes paper, sea-glass, cobalt and fine grain planes with independent HTML text/controls. One combined transparent atmosphere PNG is available now. Independent SVG/CSS layers and optional icon assets belong to later implementation; they have not been delivered as editable image layers. Decorative geometry uses broad organic contours, not straight rules, sharp section cuts or boxed cards. Static appearance is the baseline; any movement needs reduced-motion behavior and testing later.

Font lens: short Fraunces display headings, Manrope functional/body content, clear weight/size hierarchy. Generated lettering approximates this direction. Use the licensed Google Fonts distributions with retained notices in the build, and test actual wrapping, zoom, fallback and text contrast. The master palette in [DESIGN-DIRECTION.md](DESIGN-DIRECTION.md) governs alternate panel suggestions.

Future crops should keep subjects readable at real mobile widths and preserve comfortable whitespace; use responsive optimized formats, not entire unoptimized boards. Image dimensions alone establish neither site speed nor responsive layout. Never bake essential service scope, field labels, status, limitations or calls to action into the asset image.

Receipt-inspired content is the explanatory structure: observation, finding, approval, work performed, limits, next decision and responsible role. Desktop v2 shows a fictional assessment-only example. A later mobile continuation must preserve that meaning rather than dropping approval or limits to fit decorative circles. Payment, invoice and repaired-system status remain distinct.
