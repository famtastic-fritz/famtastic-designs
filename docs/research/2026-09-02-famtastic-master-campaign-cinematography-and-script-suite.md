## 3. Cinematography & Still Photography Cookbooks (Real Craft in Action)

### Recipe A: The Master Stylist (Intimate Physical Craft)
```text
PROMPT:
Cinematic, tactile commercial photograph of a master hair stylist in her early 30s intensely focused as she performs a precision shear cut in a sleek, modern boutique salon. Dynamic motion blur on the tips of the shears catching warm tungsten mirror lights. Atmosphere has soft ambient haze, polished dark obsidian stone surfaces, with a subtle lime-chartreuse rim glow on her shoulders. Shot on ARRI Alexa Mini LF with Cooke Anamorphic 65mm T2.3 lens, shallow depth of field, authentic skin pores and hair texture, high dynamic range. Natural negative space on the upper left for text overlay.

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS:
text, logo, watermark, artificial plastic skin, stiff pose, cartoon, oversaturated neon, cluttered background, distorted hands, extra fingers.
```

```text

High-impact cinematic photography of a skilled master mechanic emerging from underneath a lifted performance vehicle in a spotless, high-tech auto garage. Crisp rim lighting, volumetric shadows, Hasselblad H6D-100c, 85mm f/1.4 lens, 8k resolution, razor-sharp detail on metal textures and tools. Left side composed with clean negative space.

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS:
blurry faces, fake tools, messy unreadable compositions, cartoon styling, low resolution, stock photo smiles, deformed anatomy.
```
