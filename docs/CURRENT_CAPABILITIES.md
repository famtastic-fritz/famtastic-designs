# FAMtasticDesigns.com Current Capabilities

**Capability baseline:** August 19, 2026
**Purpose:** Define what FAMtastic Designs can credibly design, build, integrate, operate, or productize today. This is a capability map—not a product catalog and not a claim that every capability is fully automated in production.

## Core capability

FAMtastic Designs designs and engineers connected digital business systems that acquire customers, accept payments, organize delivery, support customers, measure results, and create opportunities for retention and growth. Websites are the entry point. The deeper capability is connecting the entire customer lifecycle behind them.

## Capability map

### 1. Website strategy and engineering

- Plan, architect, design, build, rebuild, and improve business websites around measurable goals.
- Create responsive, mobile-first interfaces, reusable components, structured content, forms, integrations, accessibility foundations, and performance-conscious experiences.
- **Maturity:** Demonstrated and operational.

### 2. Headless and decoupled web architecture

- Use Drupal as the operational backend while React provides branded public and customer-facing experiences.
- Separate content, commerce, identity, workflow, and presentation without exposing backend terminology to customers.
- **Maturity:** Demonstrated and operational.

### 3. Drupal implementation and customization

- Configure Drupal as a content platform, customer database, staff interface, workflow engine, and integration hub.
- Build custom modules, entities, APIs, permissions, roles, dashboards, migrations, runbooks, and operational tooling.
- **Maturity:** Demonstrated and operational.

### 4. Ecommerce and payment systems

- Model products, bundles, add-ons, configurable inclusions, customer-specific pricing, discounts, checkout, orders, receipts, and entitlements.
- Integrate Stripe securely and support one-time payments, recurring-billing architecture, failure handling, refunds, cancellations, and renewals.
- **Maturity:** Built with live infrastructure; continued transaction and lifecycle proof remains appropriate.

### 5. Customer portals

- Deliver branded account experiences for projects, files, approvals, purchases, services, domains, hosting, support, education, referrals, and growth recommendations.
- Design portal experiences for mobile-first use, long-term retention, self-service, and contextual upselling.
- **Maturity:** Demonstrated and operational, with continued experience refinement.

### 6. Customer identity and account architecture

- Create durable customer identities linked to individuals, businesses, memberships, roles, orders, projects, services, and communication preferences.
- Support email verification, password recovery, secure sessions, account-scoped authorization, prospect conversion, and duplicate prevention.
- **Maturity:** Demonstrated and operational.

### 7. Lead capture and sales operations

- Capture leads from quote forms, contact forms, referrals, campaigns, organic traffic, and personalized proof experiences.
- Track ownership, stages, response deadlines, follow-ups, qualification, proposals, outcomes, and nurture eligibility.
- **Maturity:** Strong foundation; full operational automation and escalation proof is still being expanded.

### 8. Intake and recommendation systems

- Build detailed discovery and intake flows covering goals, audience, content, functionality, integrations, commerce, automation, support, and operational needs.
- Use answers to guide scoping, recommendations, pricing treatment, project setup, entitlements, and add-on questionnaires instead of forcing every customer into one package.
- **Maturity:** Demonstrated and operational.

### 9. Support and customer communications

- Provide portal-based support threads, project conversations, status changes, file exchange, ownership, and service-linked assistance.
- Design case workflows with categories, priority, assignments, response targets, notifications, escalation, resolution, reopening, and satisfaction checks.
- **Maturity:** Core capability exists; complete two-way mailbox and advanced case automation remain in progress.

### 10. Email automation and notifications

- Send customer acknowledgments, staff alerts, verification messages, onboarding guidance, receipts, intake reminders, project updates, renewal notices, and approved retention messages.
- Separate transactional communications from promotional consent and support suppression, delivery events, bounce, complaint, retry, and failure monitoring.
- **Maturity:** Outbound and workflow foundations are demonstrated; complete reply ingestion and provider-level failure proof remain to be finished.

### 11. Business-process automation

- Turn manual customer work into defined states, deadlines, triggers, scheduled workers, reminders, retries, escalations, handoffs, and auditable histories.
- Design idempotent automation with dead-letter handling, worker health, exception summaries, and administrator controls.
- **Maturity:** Strategically strong and partially operational; full launch-grade orchestration is still being completed.

### 12. Analytics and reporting

- Implement website analytics, campaign tracking, conversion events, lead and sales metrics, response-time reporting, operational dashboards, and management views.
- Provide separate marketing analytics and operational command-center experiences, plus entitlement-controlled customer analytics.
- **Maturity:** Foundations are implemented; production data visibility and end-to-end event validation require continuing verification.

### 13. SEO foundations

- Implement structured metadata, canonical URLs, XML sitemaps, robots directives, schema markup, crawlability, internal linking, content structure, performance, and analytics measurement.
- Support ongoing SEO evaluation rather than treating SEO as a one-time checkbox.
- **Maturity:** Demonstrated foundation with continuing content and technical optimization.

### 14. Content publishing and education systems

- Create structured blogs, FAQs, help content, service documentation, topic subscriptions, and personalized educational recommendations.
- Connect content to owned services, customer questions, project stage, and relevant next actions.
- **Maturity:** Demonstrated foundation with room for deeper personalization.

### 15. AI-enabled customer experiences

- Plan and integrate AI agents, assistants, guided intake, knowledge experiences, documentation, usage support, and customer-facing automation.
- Connect AI features to real business workflows, permissions, content, analytics, escalation, and service delivery.
- **Maturity:** Strategically credible and technically supported; each AI use case requires its own implementation and proof.

### 16. Campaign systems

- Build campaign landing experiences, offer presentation, attribution, consent capture, follow-up, qualification, checkout routing, and conversion measurement.
- Support direct, organic, referral, and personalized paths without making a campaign token the permanent customer identity.
- **Maturity:** Demonstrated foundation with campaign-specific operational refinement remaining.

### 17. Personalized proof and pre-sale experiences

- Create secure, expiring, revocable proof links and tailored demonstrations that help prospects see the proposed outcome before buying.
- Track proof delivery, viewing, responses, revisions, approvals, conversion, and safe transition into permanent customer records.
- **Maturity:** Demonstrated and operational.

### 18. Hosting and domain lifecycle management

- Track customer-owned domains, existing-domain connections, DNS, SSL, hosting activation, included service periods, renewals, transfer details, grace periods, and suspension states.
- Separate domain and hosting entitlements and preserve the distinction between customer ownership and FAMtastic-managed infrastructure.
- **Maturity:** Lifecycle architecture is established; registrar automation and complete recurring-provider proof remain incomplete.

### 19. Deployment and production operations

- Build isolated customer sites, manage configuration and environment differences, deploy safely, verify production behavior, roll back when necessary, and document repeatable procedures.
- Maintain release logs, operational runbooks, security checks, cache procedures, and deployment evidence.
- **Maturity:** Demonstrated and operational.

### 20. Quality assurance and proof engineering

- Perform functional, logic, security, responsive, mobile, integration, and lifecycle QA.
- Create synthetic customer journeys, acceptance matrices, evidence artifacts, failure-path tests, cleanup controls, and launch gates that distinguish local proof, provider proof, production smoke proof, and unresolved blockers.
- **Maturity:** Strong capability with the unified evidence runner still being matured.

### 21. Productization and system design

- Turn proven delivery processes into repeatable service systems, implementation playbooks, configurable product definitions, onboarding pipelines, capability records, and saleable reference architectures.
- Capture product-specific promises, inclusions, exclusions, intake requirements, entitlements, support expectations, renewal behavior, legal language, QA requirements, and proof standards.
- **Maturity:** Actively demonstrated through FAMtastic Designs itself and ready for continued formalization.

### 22. Research-first AI creative previews and campaign worlds

- Turn a normalized brief and sourced market/cultural research into one deep
  social-presence direction or a six-direction website comparison with
  deliberate typography, pattern, texture, material depth, original imagery,
  responsive prototypes, screenshots, prompt/model/cost ledgers, browser QA,
  and visual review.
- Publish a two-surface campaign system: an immersive, functional public
  audience experience plus a separate Lab DNA case study for process,
  telemetry, QA, evidence, and attributed intake. Keep both separate from
  customer projects, prices, approvals, payments, and Site Studio state.
- Tune research depth, direction mix, image count, model/worker class,
  FAMtastic intensity, typography experimentation, texture density, motion,
  and repair budget while preserving truth, rights, accessibility, ownership,
  approval, and publishing guardrails.
- Preserve one Build DNA record for each qualifying run: the actual
  provider/model status, prompts, normalized inputs, outputs, hashes, timing,
  cost status, fallback, reviewer decision, filesystem evidence, Drupal
  retrieval projection, and Site Studio handoff lineage. Missing telemetry is
  explicitly labeled rather than backfilled from memory.
- **Maturity:** Creative and technical contracts are locally proven; one public
  interactive experience and its separate Lab DNA companion are production
  proven on desktop and phone. The Gemini Flash Lite reference-led route is
  provider-proven for one low-cost story sequence; consistent timing and
  unattended independent quality remain pending a second unrelated
  certification run.

### 23. Governed media-routing lanes

- Use HyperFrames for deliberate motion compositions built from approved proof
  or campaign assets; use MoneyPrinterTurbo only for draft-first narrated
  social/video assemblies. Neither tool may publish, send, price, or alter a
  customer project on its own.
- Keep image-volume providers interchangeable. ACI AI is a candidate reported
  by the owner, not a verified cost or rights claim, until its API, terms,
  quota, output quality, and receipt are benchmarked against the same brief.
- **Maturity:** HyperFrames is installed and MoneyPrinterTurbo is evaluated;
  neither has yet completed a FAMtastic production media run.
### 24. Campaign strategy and demand creation

- Translate an approved offer into a campaign thesis, audience, objections, hooks, learning journey, landing page, blog series, FAQs, email sequence, social moments, CTAs, and attribution plan.
- Maintain one offer truth across every channel so generated content does not invent scope, pricing, proof, or promises.
- **Maturity:** Demonstrated through the 17-day, 68-moment Web Basics campaign and its supporting content system.

### 25. Branded creative and short-form video production

- Produce campaign-specific graphics, photoreal scenarios, motion graphics, vertical videos, captions, platform-safe crops, thumbnails, scripts, and channel-native variants.
- Use provider-neutral Remotion templates as the repeatable baseline and selectively test presenter-led AI video when a human face improves trust.
- **Maturity:** Local branded image and Remotion production are proven; the first controlled HeyGen presenter comparison is in provider generation.

### 26. Social publishing and campaign operations

- Prepare, approve, schedule, publish, and verify media-rich posts while preserving account-specific copy, working destinations, UTMs, delivery evidence, and failure states.
- Operate a mobile-first Drupal campaign command center for content readiness, channel status, publishing exceptions, traffic, leads, and sales.
- **Maturity:** Facebook Page and founder-profile photo publishing are provider-proven. Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and X remain channel-specific connection and delivery gates.

### 27. AI-assisted marketing production

- Route research, drafting, QA, image creation, video production, repurposing, and classification across cloud and appropriately sized local models without allowing models to approve claims or publish autonomously.
- Compare paid presenter tools against open and programmable formats using quality, revision effort, speed, brand control, and cost per approved asset.
- **Maturity:** The governed production architecture is locally proven; individual providers and models retain separate evidence classifications.

## Maturity definitions

- **Demonstrated and operational:** Implemented and usable, with evidence appropriate to its risk.
- **Built but still requiring complete production proof:** Architecture and implementation exist, but one or more provider, lifecycle, or live-path proofs remain.
- **Strategically credible but not yet fully productized:** FAMtastic has the architecture and delivery ability, but the capability still needs standardized packaging, onboarding, pricing, or repeatable proof.

## How to use this record

- Shape offers without confusing capabilities with products.
- Identify what needs stronger proof before making a public promise.
- Guide campaigns, portal features, customer education, add-ons, and future case studies.
- Update this record when a capability is added, materially changed, proven, restricted, or retired.
