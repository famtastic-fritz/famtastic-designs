# Full Blog Logic Audit — Initial Findings

## Status

- Total published articles: 80.
- 55 Cents a Day series: eight articles deeply rewritten, fact-checked, scope-
  checked, visually checked, and production-verified.
- Remaining series: 72 articles structurally and technically validated, but not
  yet accepted as line-by-line editorially complete.

## Material finding

The remaining 72 articles share a generic long-form body framework. Forty-eight
long paragraphs are repeated in more than one post, and several paragraphs are
repeated across all 72. The repeated framework includes a Drupal/React
architecture paragraph even when the reader's question does not require that
implementation detail.

This does not prove every statement is false. It does mean the library is too
templated to claim that every article has passed a topic-specific logic review.
The campaign correction demonstrated the risk: structurally valid content can
still imply the wrong deliverable or interrupt the reader with irrelevant
technology.

## Required remediation

Rewrite and accept one series at a time:

1. Website Packages Explained
2. Small-Business Website Strategy
3. Website Lead Capture
4. Lead Response and Follow-Up
5. Ecommerce and Post-Purchase
6. Customer Portal Experience
7. Small-Business Automation
8. Website Analytics and Decisions
9. AI Website Agents

For each series:

- match every article to one search intent and reader decision;
- remove generic paragraphs that do not serve that intent;
- verify every technical, product, policy, comparative, and numeric claim;
- keep platform architecture only where it is materially useful;
- check product scope against canonical Commerce terms;
- add examples appropriate to the article rather than repeating one framework;
- verify internal links, CTA fit, visual fit, and mobile presentation;
- preserve a dated audit record before production publication is considered
  editorially complete.

The articles remain published; this audit does not authorize unpublishing or
changing commercial terms. It records the quality gap accurately so the next
rewrite is deliberate rather than another blind bulk generation pass.
