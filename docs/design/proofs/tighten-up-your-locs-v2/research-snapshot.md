# Tighten Up Your Locs — research snapshot

Prepared 2026-09-04 for a private design proof. This is the client-facing explanation of the direction, not a claim that the concepts are a live site.

## What Shay told us

- Primary outcome: more retightening rebooks.
- Audience: people at every Sisterlock stage—considering, payment/planning, tester, establishment, beginner, and maturing.
- Area: Port St. Lucie / Treasure Coast.
- Priority: scheduling must be easier when an opening appears unexpectedly; a Google Calendar connection is desired, but not currently connected.
- Booking choice: compare Booksy and request-to-book; keep payment customer-owned and approval-gated.
- Brand: royal blue and rich emerald/ruby tones; **not** flashy or overly technical; photo-led.
- Content to establish: work she started and maintains, services, policies aligned to Booksy, and authorized reviews only.

## Public research observed

The current public Booksy location listing (researched 2026-09-04) identifies a Port St. Lucie listing for *Tighten Up Your Locks* and shows Sisterlock Retightening, Consultation, and Sisterlock Maintenance. Public listing details can change, so service names, prices, address, policies, availability, and the exact business spelling must be confirmed by Shay before launch. The provided Booksy address is used only as a bridge, never as an integration claim.

## Design conclusion

This should not be a generic salon brochure. The strongest differentiator in the intake is **care continuity**: one journey across the client's Sisterlock stages, maintained with a clear retightening rhythm. The most practical growth mechanism is **owner-controlled availability**—an opening should create an easy request, not an imaginary live calendar.

## Direction rationale

1. **The Retightening Ledger** organizes care as an elegant personal record. It makes the rebook reason visible and lowers uncertainty for every stage.
2. **The Appointment Room** treats changing availability as the center of the experience. It is designed around a simple “request this opening” action that stays honest until Shay accepts it.
3. **The Established Archive** uses slow, photo-led editorial storytelling to make expertise feel lasting and personal. It is the right choice if vetted portfolio assets become the proof anchor.

## Truth labels in all concepts

- “Private concept visual” means generated concept media, not client work.
- “Request a time” means an owner-reviewed request—no calendar placement yet.
- “Book through Booksy” is an external bridge only; no scraping, sync, or payment claim.
- Reviews, outcomes, actual portfolio, address, hours, policies, and pricing require owner-confirmed source material before launch.
