# Pricing & Marketing Strategy (T4)

**Status**: working doc — CEO maintains; Fritz decides.
**Created**: 2026-08-22

## Pricing architecture today
- **$199 starter site** — entry product, proven test-provider checkout path.
- **55¢/day plan** — affordability wedge (~$200/yr, hosting year one included per lead-to-launch assumptions).
- **Business Email Setup add-on** — exists in intake + portal; template for the add-on motion.
- Hosting renewals begin month 13 — the built-in recurring base.

## The ladder (design target)
```
55¢/day  →  $199 site  →  ADD-ONs ($50–300: email setup, care plan, blog pack…)  →  GROWTH (booking, commerce, AI agents)  →  RECURRING (hosting yr2+, managed ops)
```
Rule from PRODUCT_PIPELINE scoring: at equal scores, recurring beats one-off. Every customer interaction should surface exactly ONE next rung, not the whole catalog.

## Campaign strategy for the 300 leads (waves, gated)
| Wave | Size | Purpose | Acceptance to pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 5 internal/synthetic | Prove pipeline end-to-end incl. mail receipts | Zero dead letters; selection notifications fire |
| 1 | 20 | Real outreach, highest-fit leads | ≥40% open, ≥10% click, ≥1 paid order, support SLA met |
| 2 | 80 | Scale what worked; A/B subject+offer framing | CPA known; unsubscribe <2% |
| 3 | remaining ~200 | Full campaign w/ social amplification (T2 running) | Revenue > send cost ×3 |

Prereqs before Wave 1: AUTONOMOUS_CUSTOMER_SERVICE Phase A complete (mail trustworthy), Postiz/GA4 attribution live. **No wave sends without Fritz's gate approval.**

## Positioning (public-facing story to sharpen)
"Proof first, pay after you see it": customer sees three real website proofs before paying — that's the differentiator vs every cheap-site competitor. The 55¢ price opens the door; the proof moment closes it.

## Measurement loop
Weekly CEO report must include: orders by source/campaign, add-on attach rate, wave metrics when active, top blog/social performers by attributed visits. Strategy changes happen in this file with a dated change log — not in Slack memory.
