# Web Basics: 55 Cents a Day Campaign Contract

## Campaign idea

There may be a hundred reasons a business still does not have a website. Cost
is not one of them. Period.

The $199 Web Basics price averages to approximately $0.545 per day over 365
days, which is presented as “about 55 cents a day.” This is a value comparison,
not a daily payment plan. Checkout collects the one-time $199 price.

## Canonical offer details

- One focused one-page or landing-page business website.
- Mobile-ready implementation and one primary customer action.
- One year of basic FAMtastic-managed hosting included.
- Either first-year registration of an available new domain when needed, or
  connection of a customer-owned existing domain.
- The customer owns the domain; FAMtastic manages the hosting infrastructure.
- After the included year, basic hosting currently renews at $9.99 per month
  unless canceled under the disclosed terms.
- A new domain renews separately as an annual prepaid charge. Existing-domain
  customers do not receive a new-domain renewal charge.
- Ecommerce, custom applications, complex integrations, large catalogs, and
  broader multi-page requirements are assessed and scoped separately.

## Funnel

Campaign or organic visitor → campaign education → buy or needs-led assessment
→ customer account → Commerce checkout → website intake → fit/scope confirmation
→ build → review → launch → portal delivery/support → relevant growth path.

The intake must never default every website request to $199. It gathers enough
information to recommend Web Basics, a broader package, a custom scope, or a
documented customer-specific price without changing the public catalog.

## Claim and approval boundaries

- Do not describe the offer as “55-cent daily billing.”
- Do not promise a universal launch time unless the product contract is updated
  and approved. Timing begins after required intake and customer materials are
  complete and is confirmed in the project.
- Do not imply a new domain is available until availability is checked.
- Do not silently add ecommerce or custom-system work to the defined bundle.
- Product price, renewal price, recurring consent, legal terms, and promotional
  email sends remain governed approval points.
- No revenue, ranking, traffic, conversion, or customer-result claim is allowed
  without recorded evidence.

## Content coverage

The canonical demand manifest contains an eight-article 55 Cents a Day series
and an eight-article package-explanation series. Every service and package page
links to relevant Drupal-owned education. Campaign visuals are stored in the
frontend asset library and identified in each article's editorial metadata.
