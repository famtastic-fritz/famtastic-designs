# Approval gates

## May run autonomously

- Research, outlines, drafts, metadata, taxonomy proposals, FAQ drafts, CTA mappings, internal-link plans, pricing hypotheses, Drupal draft seeding, local builds, test-environment checks, and evidence reports.
- Corrections that preserve already approved meaning, price, scope, and publication state.

## Requires Fritz's explicit approval

- Changing a live product price or discount policy.
- Creating or changing recurring customer charges.
- Publishing contractual promises, guarantees, or final legal language.
- Sending promotional campaigns to real recipients.
- Spending money on advertising or third-party distribution.
- Broadly publishing a new content batch.
- Claiming provider or production proof that the capability registry does not support.

## Fail closed

If an action crosses a gate, prepare the complete release package and stop before the consequential mutation. Report the exact decision required; do not replace it with a guess.
