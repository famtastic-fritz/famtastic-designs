# Brief — what-website-maintenance-actually-covers

## Target reader
A customer who already has a live site (via FAMtastic or elsewhere) and is
deciding whether an ongoing maintenance plan is worth paying for monthly, or
is unclear on what "maintenance" even means versus a redesign or new feature.

## Search intent
Investigative: "what does website maintenance include", "do I need a
maintenance plan for my website", "website maintenance vs redesign". They
want a plain scope boundary, not vague reassurance.

## Key takeaway
Website maintenance is a specific, bounded monthly service — update checks,
backup verification, monitoring for anything broken, and small content
touches (hours, staff names, a service line) — not a catch-all for new pages,
redesigns, or marketing campaigns. Keeping those separate is what keeps the
monthly price honest instead of turning into an open-ended retainer.
