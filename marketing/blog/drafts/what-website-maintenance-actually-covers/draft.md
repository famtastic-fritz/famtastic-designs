# What Website Maintenance Actually Covers (and What It Doesn't)

"Maintenance" gets used loosely, so here is ours in plain terms: every month we check your site's updates, verify backups are really there, watch for anything broken, and handle small content touches - hours changed, a staff name, a service line.

What it doesn't include: new page builds, redesigns, or marketing campaigns. Those are separate packages so the monthly price stays honest. The point of a care plan is simple - the site you paid for stays the site you paid for.
