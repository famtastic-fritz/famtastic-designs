# Proof-First Websites: See Three Real Designs Before You Pay

Most affordable-website offers ask for your money first and show you the result later. That order is exactly why owners are skeptical — and they are right to be.

FAMtastic Designs runs the opposite direction: proofs come first, payment follows your decision.

## How the flow works

1. **You describe the business.** A guided website request collects what matters: what you do, who you serve, what a good customer looks like.
2. **Three real proofs get built.** Not mockup grids of gray boxes — three distinct website directions built from your actual business information.
3. **You pick a direction.** The selection step is a real fork in the process, not a formality.
4. **Checkout follows your selection.** Only after you have seen the work and chosen a direction does payment enter the picture.

## Why this order matters to you

Design taste is personal. A price tag cannot tell you whether a builder understands your business — but three concrete proofs can. When you choose from real options built on real content, the risk of "I paid and got something I hate" drops to nearly zero, because hating all three means you never paid anything.

## What it is not

It is not free unlimited design labor, and it is not a subscription trap. The Web Basics package is a defined one-time scope with first-year hosting included; renewal terms are stated on the package page before you ever reach checkout.

## Who it fits best

Local service businesses that need to be findable and contactable — trades, clinics, food, studios, professional services — and that want a straight answer to "what will my website actually look like?" before any money moves.
