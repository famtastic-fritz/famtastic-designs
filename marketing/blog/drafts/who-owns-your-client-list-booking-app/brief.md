# Brief — who-owns-your-client-list-booking-app

## Target reader
Independent service operator who books through an app and needs a practical, non-legal way to understand their client-data position.

## Search intent
Informational: “who owns client data booking app” / “salon client list ownership” / “marketplace app customer data.” The reader needs a short audit of export, permission, and account-end terms.

## Key takeaway
Seeing client records in a booking app is not the same as controlling a reusable client list; the operator should check what exports, whether off-platform contact is allowed, and what happens when the account ends.
