# Why Independent Hair Stylists Are Invisible Outside the App

## The search that never happens

Someone new to your area searches "hair stylist near me." Your name doesn't come up — not because you aren't good, not because you aren't busy, but because there is nothing of yours for a search engine to find. No page in your business name, no address of your own, nothing that exists outside one booking app and your Instagram messages.

You're not missing bookings because you're bad at your job. You are missing them because outside that app, you are a ghost.

## What being a digital ghost actually costs

This is not the same problem as app fees. You may already know exactly what a booking app costs you every month, and you may be perfectly fine with it. This is a quieter cost, and it never shows up on a statement.

- **People new to the area cannot find you.** A move-in, a referral passed along without your handle, a search from someone who does not already follow you — all of it goes to whoever actually appears in the results.
- **A stranger has no work of yours to browse.** A marketplace profile shows a few photos in a fixed template. It does not show your range, your specialty, or why you are worth booking over the other stylists listed on the same screen.
- **You own none of the traffic.** Traffic is just the word for people arriving at a page. Everyone who found you through the app found you *through the app*, and if that app changes how listings are ordered, that channel changes with it.

## The part that surprises people

Here's the honest version, because the simple version isn't quite right.

Your booking-app profile may well appear in Google. Marketplaces want to be found, and their profile pages often are. So "there is nothing for Google to find" is too strong a way to put it.

But being *on* a page that ranks is not the same as *having* one. The web address belongs to the app. The layout belongs to the app. Whatever credit a search engine gives that page for being useful goes to the app's name, not to yours. And the other stylists on that platform sit on the same page, competing for the same click.

A profile inside someone else's app can be genuinely visible and still not be yours. That distinction deserves its own article, and it gets one next in this series.

## What "yours" actually means

Two plain definitions, because these words get thrown around a lot:

- A **domain** is your address on the web — something like styledbysarah.com. You register it, you renew it, and it points wherever you tell it to point.
- **Indexing** is what a search engine does when it reads a page and files it away so it can be shown to someone later. A page has to exist and be readable before it can be indexed at all.

Own the first, and the second becomes possible. That's most of the idea.
