# Brief — how-much-do-you-charge-dms-costs-bookings

## Target reader
Service-business owner who receives repeat price questions in social DMs and wants to reduce friction without committing to a one-size-fits-all quote.

## Search intent
Informational: “answering price questions in dms” / “should I post prices on my website” / “customers asking how much instagram.” The reader needs practical price-boundary patterns, not a claim that public pricing guarantees conversion.

## Key takeaway
“DM for pricing” creates extra decisions and waits before a booking; a floor, range with a named variable, or stated pricing condition can qualify a conversation without making a binding quote.
