# Brief — website-answers-price-questions-before-asked

## Target reader
Service-business owner who wants a simple page and form to answer repeated price, service, and availability questions before an inquiry starts.

## Search intent
Informational how-to: “website quote request automation small business” / “automatic quote estimate website” / “intake form for service business.” The reader needs a plain scope for a useful page, not a promise of automatic binding quotes.

## Key takeaway
Most price-question automation begins with writing repeated answers onto a page; a focused form collects only the variable needed for a real quote, while routing and notifications remain a separate scoped system.
