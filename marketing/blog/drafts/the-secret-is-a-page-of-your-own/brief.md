# Brief — the-secret-is-a-page-of-your-own

## Target reader
A business owner who has recently noticed a peer, in the same or an adjacent trade, now sends people to a website instead of a profile or a booking link, and is quietly wondering what changed and what it costs.

## Key takeaway
The "secret" a peer seems to have found is not a result — it is a product. A page of your own, at a domain you actually hold, is a structurally different thing from a booking profile or a link-in-bio page, and it costs $199 for the whole first year: one page, one year of hosting, the domain. No revenue, booking count, or ranking is ever implied by the presence of the page, and the dramatizations built to illustrate this moment say so explicitly, on screen, every time.
