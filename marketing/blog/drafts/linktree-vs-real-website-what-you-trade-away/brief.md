# Brief — linktree-vs-real-website-what-you-trade-away

## Target reader
Small-business owner using a link-in-bio page who wants to know whether it is enough for a business presence.

## Search intent
Informational comparison: “linktree vs website” / “linktree for business pros and cons” / “is linktree enough for a business.” The reader needs a plain tradeoff, not a demand to delete the tool they already use.

## Key takeaway
A link-in-bio page remains useful for routing social visitors, but an owned website is the place that can answer service, price, location, and contact questions at an address the business controls.
