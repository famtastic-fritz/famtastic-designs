# Brief — does-google-index-your-booking-app-profile

## Target reader
Any business whose only findable page is a profile inside a booking app or marketplace — stylists, barbers, trainers, cleaners, mobile detailers, tradespeople. Beginner on search vocabulary. Often already suspects the answer is "no" and is wrong about why.

## Search intent
Mechanism-seeking: "does Google index Booksy profiles", "will my marketplace listing show up in search", "why doesn't my business show up on Google". They want to know how the machinery works. This is the post in the series with the most value to a reader who will never buy anything.

## Key takeaway
A booking-app profile is usually crawled and indexed — the common assumption that it is invisible to search is wrong. What a profile cannot do is be steered: you do not control the page title, you cannot add structured data describing your business, you cannot choose where a visitor goes next, and you cannot keep the address if the platform changes it. Search reputation accrues to the domain that hosts the page, which is the platform's, not yours. A page on your own domain puts those four controls back in your hands without requiring you to leave the app.
