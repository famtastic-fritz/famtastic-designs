# Brief — why-running-business-on-gmail-and-linktree-costs-revenue

## Target reader
Local service business owner (stylist, mobile detailer, tradesperson) currently booking through Instagram/Facebook DMs, using a @gmail.com address, and/or a Linktree-style link-in-bio page instead of their own website.

## Search intent
Investigative/comparison: "do I need a website if I have Instagram" / "linktree vs website for small business" / "professional email vs gmail for business". They want to understand what they're actually losing, not a generic sales pitch.

## Key takeaway
A @gmail.com address and a Linktree page are both rented land: they carry no domain credibility, no owned customer data, and in Linktree's case often a booking/affiliate layer that takes a cut or adds friction. An owned domain plus a real site removes the middleman step (a customer messaging "how much?" and waiting for a DM reply) and replaces it with a page that answers the question and can intake/estimate automatically.
