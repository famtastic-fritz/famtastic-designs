# Do You Guarantee Google Rankings?

No. Nobody honest does. If a vendor tells you they can guarantee a specific
Google ranking or "get you to page 1," that's a claim no one can actually
back up — and it's worth treating as a warning sign, not a selling point.

## Why no one can actually promise this

Google's ranking algorithm isn't something any outside vendor controls. It
weighs your competitors, the searcher's location, the exact wording and
intent of their search, the device they're using, and constant algorithm
changes that shift week to week. A vendor who "guarantees" a position is
either promising something outside their control or quietly narrowing the
promise later — a specific search term nobody actually uses, a location
with no real competition, a ranking that lasts one week. None of that helps
your business.

## What actually can be built, and verified

What a vendor can honestly do is build the real technical foundation search
engines need to evaluate a site at all, and then show you proof it's there
— not a promise, something you can check yourself:

- **Structured data** that tells search engines exactly what your business is and where you serve, so you're eligible to be considered in the first place.
- **Correct, matching business profiles** across Google, Maps, and relevant directories — the plumbing local search depends on.
- **Analytics connected and working**, so you can see real traffic and real behavior instead of taking anyone's word for it.

That's capability you can verify, not an outcome anyone can promise. The
difference matters: one is something you can check today, the other is a
claim you can only find out was false months from now.
