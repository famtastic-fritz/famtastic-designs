# Brief — do-you-guarantee-google-rankings

## Target reader
A prospective or existing customer asking directly, in a sales conversation
or a search, whether FAMtastic (or any vendor) can guarantee a specific
Google ranking or "page 1" placement for their business.

## Search intent
Direct question/trust-check: "does [vendor] guarantee google rankings",
"can a website guarantee first page google", "is a ranking guarantee real".
They may have already heard a competitor promise this and want a second,
honest opinion before they decide who to trust.

## Key takeaway
No honest vendor can guarantee a specific Google ranking — rankings are
controlled by Google's own algorithm, which factors in competitors,
location, search intent, device, and constant algorithm changes, none of
which any vendor controls. What FAMtastic can do, and does, is build the
real technical foundation that search engines need to evaluate a site fairly:
structured data, correct business profiles, and analytics that prove what's
actually happening — verifiable capability, not a promised outcome. Anyone
who promises a specific ranking position is telling you something no one
can actually deliver.
