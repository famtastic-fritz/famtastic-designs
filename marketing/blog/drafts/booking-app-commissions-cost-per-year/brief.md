# Brief — booking-app-commissions-cost-per-year

## Target reader
Independent service operator using a booking platform who has seen a fee line but has not translated it into a yearly decision.

## Search intent
Commercial investigation: “booking app commission fees” / “salon booking app fees” / “booking commission vs subscription.” The reader needs a neutral way to calculate their own cost without a platform-rate claim.

## Key takeaway
The useful calculation is not a generic platform rate: total the operator's own monthly fee line, annualize it, then separate fees paid for introductions from fees paid on repeat clients.
