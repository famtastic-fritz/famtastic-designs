# Brief — how-local-customers-find-your-business-online

## Target reader
A local service business owner (contractor, salon, clinic, restaurant) who
has a website already but isn't showing up when someone nearby searches for
what they do — or shows up inconsistently across Google, Maps, and other
directories.

## Search intent
Informational/how-to: "how do customers find my business online", "why
doesn't my business show up on Google", "local SEO for small business". They
want to understand the mechanism, not a jargon-heavy sales pitch.

## Key takeaway
Local search is three separate, connected layers — structured data that
tells search engines what you are and where you serve, business profiles
that carry your hours and map pin, and analytics that prove which layer
actually brought the visit. Skipping any one layer makes the other two
underperform, and most "we don't show up" problems trace back to one missing
or inconsistent layer, not a mystery algorithm.
