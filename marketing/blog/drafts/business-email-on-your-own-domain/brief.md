# Brief — business-email-on-your-own-domain

## Target reader
A small-business owner who already has (or is about to get) a website but is
still sending invoices, quotes, and customer replies from a @gmail.com,
@yahoo.com, or @outlook.com address. Often the same reader who just read
about the DM gap or the $199 package and is now looking at the upsell list.

## Search intent
Investigative/how-to: "business email vs gmail", "how to set up email on my
own domain", "do I need [name]@mydomain.com". They want to know what the
switch actually involves and whether it's a big technical project or a small
one.

## Key takeaway
A business email address on your own domain (you@yourbusiness.com instead of
yourbusiness@gmail.com) is a trust signal that costs nothing to notice and
very little to fix. The work itself is a defined, bounded task — DNS records
that authorize your domain to send mail, connecting a mailbox provider, and a
send/receive test — not a redesign of anything else you own.
