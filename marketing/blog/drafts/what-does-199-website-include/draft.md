# What Does the $199 Website Actually Include?

When a website costs less than a month of most marketing budgets, the fair question is: what's the catch?

Here is the direct answer for FAMtastic Designs' Web Basics package: the price is low because the scope is defined, not because the work is thin.

## The published scope

Web Basics is a one-time $199 and includes:

- A complete small-business website built around your real content
- Mobile-ready pages that work on the phones your customers actually use
- A contact form so inquiries reach you instead of a dead end
- Your first year of basic managed hosting included
- First-year registration of a qualifying standard domain when one is needed

Everything above mirrors the live package page at famtasticdesigns.com/web/packages/web-basics — if anything there changes, that page, not this article, is the contract.

## What "defined scope" means

Defined scope is a feature. You know exactly what ships: the pages a local business needs to be findable and contactable. You are not paying for discovery workshops, custom web applications, or an ongoing agency retainer you did not ask for.

If your business outgrows the basics — booking, ecommerce, customer portals — those exist as separate packages rather than hidden upsells inside this one.

## Why you see proofs before you pay

The part that makes this different from every "cheap website" offer you have seen: before payment you review three real website proofs built from your actual business information. You pick a direction first; the build follows your selection.

That single step answers the question every buyer of an affordable site silently asks — "will it look like a template someone slapped my logo on?"

## The 55¢ framing, honestly

The 55-cents-a-day figure is the annualized comparison of the one-time $199 price across a year (about 55¢ per day). It is a way to talk about affordability, not a subscription. Hosting renewals begin in month 13 at then-current rates, which the package page states plainly.
