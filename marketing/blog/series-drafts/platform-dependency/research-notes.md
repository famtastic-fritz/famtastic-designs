# Research notes — platform-dependency series

Retrieved 2026-09-05. Sources below support the search-mechanism language in
the pillar candidate; they do not support a guarantee that a particular page
will rank or be indexed.

| Claim boundary | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Google describes crawling, indexing, and serving as separate stages. | [How Google Search works](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/how-search-works) | Use this vocabulary when explaining why a page must be discoverable and understandable. Google says it does not guarantee crawling, indexing, or serving. |
| Helpful, people-first content is the priority, rather than content made mainly to manipulate rankings. | [Creating helpful, reliable, people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) | Supports the series' practical, non-keyword-stuffed approach. |
| Descriptive titles, links, and organized content can help people and search engines understand pages. | [SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide) | Do not turn this into a ranking promise. |
| Google recommends words people use in prominent page locations and crawlable links. | [Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials) | Use to justify clear service/location/price-boundary language, not to promise traffic. |

## Excluded claims

- No published booking-platform commission rate, customer-data ownership rule,
  or off-platform-contact right is asserted without a source for that specific
  provider and account agreement.
- No traffic, conversion, crawl, index, or ranking outcome is promised.
- No FAMtastic product price, inclusions, or automation feature is stated here;
  those need a current product-catalog check immediately before publication.
