Status: `draft_review_only`

## Purpose

Help appointment-led and local-service owners keep the platforms that bring
them attention while building an owned place to answer questions, receive an
inquiry, and retain an approved contact record.

## Proposed title

**The Business You Can Reach Series**

Alternatives for owner review:

1. The Own-Your-Client-Path Series
2. The Rented Platforms, Real Business Series

The title is only a local proposal. It does not create or rename a Drupal
`blog_series` term.

## Evidence and writing rules

- Use the [research notes](research-notes.md) for the limited current search
  mechanism claims. Google does not guarantee crawling, indexing, or ranking.
- Do not quote named booking-platform rates, terms, or data-ownership rules
  without a current source for that platform.
- Do not call a price range a binding quote, or claim that posting a price
  increases bookings.
- Keep the existing booking platform in the reader's system; the content is
  about reducing single-platform dependency, not demanding a migration.

## Deliberate publication boundary

Before this can become a live ordered series, the owner must approve the title,
the exact series manifest facts, and whether the already-published pillar
changes series. Then the production taxonomy term must exist before a dry-run
can proceed. No file here is a publishing request or a substitute for those
gates.
