# AND IF IT IS?

## Campaign identity

- Public-facing name: **AND IF IT IS?**
- Descriptor: **The Unofficial Rattler Lifers Project**
- Suggested handle: **@AndIfItIs** (not registered)
- Question: **Is FAMU a cult?**
- Response: **And if it is?**
- Supporting line: **We do not graduate from the feeling.**
- Voice: affectionate, knowing, defiant, intergenerational, funny, informed
- Boundary: cultural commentary and fan pride; never literal cult behavior

## The idea

The joke works because the attachment is visible. Rattlers graduate, move away,
build careers, raise families—and still organize their calendar around games,
Homecoming, Set Friday, the Marching 100, alumni chapters, mentoring, recruiting,
giving, and coming back to the Hill. The response “And if it is?” is not a defense
of coercion. It is a playful refusal to apologize for lifelong belonging.

## Five content pillars

1. **Once a Rattler** — class-year roll calls and memories that instantly identify another Rattler.
2. **Why we come back** — games, Homecoming, the Hill, the band, the Set, food, family, and reunion.
3. **We Bragg different** — sourced excellence, alumni impact, research, service, culture, and resilience.
4. **The FAMUly keeps growing** — current students, alumni, parents, children, future Rattlers, and mentors.
5. **Pride with a purpose** — attend, mentor, recruit, join alumni efforts, and support through official channels.

## Character branding

**The Lifer** is an original serpent-spirit character representing the Rattler
who never really leaves. The character is warm, stylish, witty, seasoned, and
welcoming—not aggressive. It must never copy an official mascot, carry official
marks, wear a lettered uniform, or imply university endorsement.

Recurring character uses:

- “The Lifer remembers…” archival posts
- “The Lifer is already packed” game-week posts
- “The Lifer found another Rattler in…” alumni-city posts
- “The Lifer says bring somebody back” mentoring and reunion posts
- “The Lifer checks the source” factual corrections and official-link posts

## Social account boundary

The suggested handle avoids FAMU in the username. Every bio and landing surface
must state: “Unofficial Rattler fan commentary. Not affiliated with or endorsed
by Florida A&M University.” No social account has been created and no post has
been published by this proof.

- Shares and saves on memory/tradition posts
- Clicks to official alumni, events, and giving destinations
- Responses identifying cities and alumni chapters
- Repeat participation across game weeks and Homecoming
