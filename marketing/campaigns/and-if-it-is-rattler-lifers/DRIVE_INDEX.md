## What this baseline is

One complete, research-first social-presence direction for the unofficial
Rattler Lifers concept. It is not a six-direction website package. The package
includes the responsive hub, two original 2K images, The Lifer character,
three editable social cards, screenshots, exact prompts, research, model/cost
ledger, routing alternatives, local and live verification, and the related
architecture decision.

## Boundaries

- The audience experience is public and indexable; its roll call generates,
  persists, copies, and shares from the visitor's device without transmitting
  the entry.
- The original proof artifact remains public-unlisted for evidence continuity.
- The Lab case study is public and indexable; it is a FAMtastic process/demo
  page, not an official university or customer site.
- No social account was created and no social post was published.
- The roll-call interaction is device-local and transmits nothing.
- The campaign is unofficial and claims no Florida A&M University endorsement.
- OpenArt + GPT Image 2 is the proven image route for this run. Direct OpenAI
  Image API is documented as a same-model alternative but has not been
  authenticated or benchmarked in this repository.
