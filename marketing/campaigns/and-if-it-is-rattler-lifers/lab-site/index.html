The creative jump

Research gave us the truth. Design gave it teeth.

The idea was never simply “make something orange and green.” The research surfaced a durable cultural behavior: Rattlers carry the school with them for life. That became a provocation—“Is FAMU a cult?” “And if it is?”—and every visual choice had to reinforce the unapologetic answer.

The locked baseline

A small recipe with a big point of view.

We keep the preview fast by limiting what is expensive—not by making the design generic. Research and a creative thesis come first. Two image jobs run together. The system then assembles, tests, and records the result.

Image generation 2 parallel

GPT Image 2 through OpenArt, 315 credits total, with zero retries.

Browser gate 5 routes

Desktop and phone, three social cards, zero final overflow, broken assets, or runtime errors.

Visual review 9.0/10

Generating-agent self-review. Useful evidence, but not an independent reviewer or a guarantee for future runs.

Repeatability confidence

High on the recipe. Moderate on the promise.

The source, exact prompts, model ledger, costs, screenshots, hashes, browser assertions, visual scores, and one-command verifier are preserved. That makes the run inspectable and adjustable. It takes another unrelated brand reproducing the time and quality gates before “13 minutes every time” becomes a certified service claim.
