# Adobe Creative Suite Automation Scripts

**Campaign**: Cost Is Not The Reason ($199 Web Basics Launch)  
**Compatibility**: Adobe Photoshop 2024+, Adobe After Effects 2024+, Adobe Premiere Pro  

---

## 1. Photoshop Batch Multi-Format Exporter (`Photoshop_Batch_MultiFormat_Exporter.jsx`)

### What It Does:
Automates taking your 1:1 master campaign graphics or raw photography and exporting all 3 standard social formats in seconds:
* **1:1 Square Feed (1080x1080px)** — Instagram & Facebook Feed
* **9:16 Vertical Story/Reel (1080x1920px)** — Instagram Stories, Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts
* **16:9 Landscape (1920x1080px)** — Web banners, X (Twitter), YouTube thumbnails

### How to Run:
1. Open **Adobe Photoshop**.
2. Go to **File > Scripts > Browse...**
3. Select `Photoshop_Batch_MultiFormat_Exporter.jsx`.
4. Pick the campaign artwork image file.
5. All 3 formats are automatically cropped, optimized, and saved into an `/exports/` subfolder.

## 2. After Effects 15s Commercial Motion Builder (`AfterEffects_Cost_Objection_Motion_Graphic.jsx`)

### What It Does:
Generates a complete 15-second vertical commercial composition with timed kinetic typography, branded chartreuse `#7CFC00` glow accents, and animated call-to-action cards mapped to the official audio storyboard beats:
* **0:00 – 0:03s**: *"WHAT'S YOUR EXCUSE?"* (Scale-pop & focus)
* **0:03 – 0:07s**: *"COST IS NOT ONE OF THEM."* (Glowing pulse)
* **0:07 – 0:11s**: *"55¢ A DAY / $199 FULL FIRST YEAR."* (Kinetic slide)
* **0:11 – 0:15s**: *"STOP RENTING. START OWNING. famtasticdesigns.com"* (Brand CTA)

### How to Run:
1. Open **Adobe After Effects**.
2. Go to **File > Scripts > Run Script File...**
3. Select `AfterEffects_Cost_Objection_Motion_Graphic.jsx`.
4. The script creates the master 9:16 vertical composition `FAMtastic_15s_Cost_Is_Not_The_Reason_9x16` ready for audio and B-roll layer insertion.
