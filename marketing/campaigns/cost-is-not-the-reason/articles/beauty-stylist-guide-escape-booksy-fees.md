## How to Transition Your Booksy Clients to Your Owned Hub

Many stylists fear that leaving an app means losing bookings. The reality is that **80%+ of your bookings come from repeat loyal clients and your personal Instagram referrals**, not random marketplace searches.

Here is the simple 3-step playbook to transition your clients smoothly:

### Step 1: Launch Your Custom Branded Booking Hub
Get a dedicated, modern landing page with your name/brand (e.g., `styledbysarah.com`). It showcases your real portfolio, transparent service pricing, appointment calendar, and policies.

### Step 2: Update Your Bio & Automated Reply
Replace all third-party app links in your Instagram bio with your new custom link:
> *"Book your next VIP appointment directly on my official website: [styledbysarah.com](https://styledbysarah.com) ✨"*

### Step 3: Offer an "Owned Hub" Booking Incentive
Send a quick text or post a story to your existing clients:
> *"Hey beauties! I’ve officially launched my new website. Book your next appointment at [styledbysarah.com](https://styledbysarah.com) and get a complimentary deep conditioning treatment on your next visit!"*

Your clients will love the faster, cleaner experience, and you never pay a third-party fee on their bookings again.
