# Direct Outreach Playbooks (DM & Cold Email)

**Campaign**: Cost Is Not The Reason ($199 Web Basics / 55¢ a Day)  
**Target Profile 1**: Service businesses & beauty professionals using Booksy / StyleSeat links in bio.  
**Target Profile 2**: Local service businesses (auto, repair, detailing, pop-ups) using `@gmail.com` or `@yahoo.com`.  

---

## Playbook 1: The Booksy / Marketplace Captive Outreach (Instagram DM)

### Context & Qualification:
* Target has an active Instagram page with great work/photos.
* Bio has a Booksy / StyleSeat / Vagaro link or says "DM to book".

### Message 1 (The Respectful Insight & Hook):
> *"Hey {{Name}}! Love the work on your page—that recent {{specific style / service}} looked super clean 🔥.*
> 
> *Quick question for you: I noticed you're booking through {{Booksy/StyleSeat}}. Are you tired of paying their monthly fees and losing a cut every time a new client books?*
> 
> *We’re a Business Solutions Engineering studio (FAMtastic Designs). We build independent stylists their own custom branded booking site with hosting included for $199 for the full first year (about 55¢ a day) so you keep 100% of your earnings and own your client list.*
> 
> *I put together a quick preview of what your custom booking site could look like if you want to check it out. No pressure at all—should I send the link over?"*

### Response Handling:
* **If they say YES**: Send their signed preview link + *"Here you go: {{preview_link}}! You can see your service menu, direct calendar, and how clients book with zero app fees. Let me know what you think!"*
* **If they ask about price**: *"It’s $199 total for the entire first year—no monthly fees, no cuts taken from your appointments, and managed hosting is included. Renews at just $9.99/mo after year one."*
