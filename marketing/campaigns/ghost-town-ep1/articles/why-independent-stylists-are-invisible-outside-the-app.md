## The Search That Never Happens

Someone new to your area searches "hair stylist near me." Your name doesn't come up — not because you're not good, not because you're not busy, but because there's nothing for Google to find. No website, no page, no address it can index. Just a profile buried inside Booksy or StyleSeat, invisible to anyone who isn't already searching that specific app.

You're not missing bookings because you're bad at your job. You're missing them because outside of one app and your own Instagram DMs, you don't exist.

## What Being a "Digital Ghost" Actually Costs

This isn't the same problem as marketplace fees — you might already know exactly what Booksy or StyleSeat costs you every month. This is a different, quieter cost:

* **New-to-the-area clients can't find you.** A move-in, a referral without your handle, a Google search from someone who doesn't already follow you on Instagram — all of it goes to whichever stylist actually shows up in search.
* **You have no proof of work a stranger can browse.** A marketplace profile shows a few photos in a fixed template. It doesn't show your range, your story, or why you're worth booking over the five other stylists on the same app.
* **You own none of the traffic.** Every client who finds you through the app found you *through the app* — if it changes its algorithm, raises its take, or buries your listing, that discovery channel is gone. Nothing personally yours replaces it.
