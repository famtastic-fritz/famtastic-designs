# Ghost Town — Episode 1: "The Hair Salon Ghosts"

**Format**: 9:16, 58s master cut (+ 3 short cuts: 15s hook, 15s "DM trap," 22s before/after)
**Target**: Independent hair stylists / salon owners with no owned web presence
**Offer anchor**: FAM-FOOT-199 — $199 first year (~55¢/day), $9.99/mo after year 1
**Tone**: Direct, a little wry, not doom-y. This is a fixable problem, not a scary one.

---

## Master Script (58s)

**[0:00–0:06] Hook**
> "Search 'hair stylist near me' right now. If you're an independent stylist... are you even in the results?"

**[0:06–0:16] Problem**
> "Most independent stylists exist in exactly one place online: inside a booking app. Which means outside of that app — to Google, to a new client who just moved here, to anyone who isn't already following you — you're a ghost."

**[0:16–0:28] Reframe**
> "This isn't about what the app charges you. Even if you're fine with the fees, you're still invisible to everyone who isn't already looking for you by name."

**[0:28–0:42] Solution**
> "One page. Your own domain. Your actual portfolio, not a template thumbnail. Built and live in 48 hours, with your first year of hosting included."

**[0:42–0:52] Price**
> "$199 for the whole first year. That's about 55 cents a day. After year one, $9.99 a month if you keep it — no surprise renewal."

**[0:52–0:58] CTA**
> "Stop being a ghost. Link in bio — see your 3 design directions in 48 hours."

---

## Short Cut 1 — "The Hook" (15s)
Uses [0:00–0:16] trimmed to land the "digital ghost" line by :12.

## Short Cut 2 — "The DM Trap" (15s)
> "If the only way to book you is a DM or an app, every new client is one algorithm change away from never finding you. One owned page fixes that — for less than a dollar a day."

## Short Cut 3 — "Before & After Shock" (22s)
Split-screen: left = search result with no match / generic app listing. Right = search result showing the stylist's own page, portfolio, and direct booking link.
> "Same stylist. Same work. One of these shows up when someone searches for you — the other doesn't."

- No city name hardcoded into voiceover or on-screen text — keep it swappable per-market unless a specific city shoot (e.g. real local B-roll) is confirmed.
- Pricing on-screen text must read exactly: "$199 first year · ~55¢/day · $9.99/mo after year 1" — matches `FAM-FOOT-199` / `FAM-HOST-999` in `backend/config/famtastic-products.json`.
