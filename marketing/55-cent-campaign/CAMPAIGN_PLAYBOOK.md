## Comment response guide

- **“Is it really 55 cents per day?”** — “The purchase is one $199 payment.
  $199 divided across 365 days is about 55 cents per day; it is a value
  comparison, not daily billing.”
- **“Does it include a domain?”** — “If you need one, first-year registration
  of an available standard domain is included. If you already control a domain,
  connection is included instead. Domain renewal is separate after year one.”
- **“Does it include hosting?”** — “The first year of basic managed hosting is
  included. The campaign page explains the configured $9.99/month renewal after
  the included term and cancellation details.”
- **“Can you build my online store for $199?”** — “The $199 offer is a focused
  first business website. A store needs commerce scope. Use the assessment and
  we will recommend the smallest complete solution.”
- **“How fast?”** — “Timing is confirmed after the required intake and customer
  materials are complete. We do not advertise an unsupported universal
  turnaround.”

## Paid-test structure (requires Fritz approval before spend)

Test creative angles separately so the result is interpretable:

1. **Affordability:** 55¢-a-day character creative.
2. **Objection:** three-owner excuses creative.
3. **Trust:** customer verification creative.
4. **Transparency:** equation and included-items creative.

Use the campaign-page view as the initial optimization event until purchase
volume supports deeper optimization. Track campaign-page view, assessment
start, checkout start, and purchase separately. Do not claim that an ad or
website guarantees traffic, leads, or revenue.
