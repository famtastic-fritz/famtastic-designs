# Adobe Firefly video comparison test

**Purpose:** Compare Adobe Firefly with the approved HeyGen presenter ad and
the existing Remotion master before adding another paid subscription.

This is a controlled creative test. Preserve the approved Web Basics offer:

- $199 is a one-time price for a bounded basic website.
- “About 55 cents a day” is $199 divided by 365, not daily billing.
- First-year basic hosting is included.
- First-year registration for a new standard domain is included when needed;
  an existing domain can be connected instead.
- Ecommerce, a custom Drupal application, and unsupported turnaround promises
  are not included in the $199 claim.
- Canonical destination: `https://famtasticdesigns.com/55-cents-a-day-website`.

## Test A — Firefly Text to Avatar

Use this test to compare the closest Adobe equivalent to the HeyGen presenter.

1. Open Adobe Firefly.
2. Choose **Text to avatar (beta)**, then **Create new**.
3. Select a confident, approachable presenter and American English voice.
4. Choose a simple dark or neutral studio background. Do not ask the avatar
   generator to render the FAMtastic logo or campaign URL.
5. Select portrait/vertical output if the interface offers it. If it only
   produces landscape, export and reframe it in Adobe Express or Premiere.
6. Paste the script below.
7. Generate one version. Do not spend credits on multiple variants until the
   first result has been scored.

### Presenter script

> Still putting off your website? Maybe it feels too expensive. Maybe you do
> not have time. Maybe business seems fine without one. But missing or unclear
> information can make your business harder to trust and harder to choose.
> FAMtastic Designs can build your professional basic website for $199—that is
> about 55 cents a day for the first year. Basic hosting is included for one
> year. If you need a new standard domain, its first-year registration is
> included too. There may be a hundred reasons your business does not have a
> website. Cost is not one of them. Period. Start at FAMtasticDesigns.com.

The script is intentionally longer than the 20-second HeyGen creative. Record
the actual duration rather than speeding the voice until it sounds unnatural.

## Test B — Firefly cinematic B-roll

Firefly's Generate Video feature is better suited to short visual scenes than a
complete branded advertisement. Generate three 5-second vertical clips, then
assemble them with the presenter, captions, campaign graphics, logo, music, and
CTA in Adobe Express or Premiere.

Use **Firefly Video**, **9:16**, and the strongest available resolution.

### Clip 1 — the hesitation

> Vertical cinematic social-video shot of a real small-business bakery owner
> after closing, seated at a counter and looking at an unfinished website plan
> on a laptop. Warm practical lighting, relatable rather than gloomy, subtle
> slow camera push-in, realistic hands and screen interaction, premium
> commercial photography, generous center-safe composition, no readable text,
> no logos, no watermark, 9:16.

### Clip 2 — the missed trust moment

> Vertical cinematic close-up of a potential customer searching on a phone for
> a local bakery, hesitating because the business has no clear official website
> or trustworthy information. Natural phone behavior, authentic neighborhood
> setting, shallow depth of field, restrained camera movement, realistic hands,
> premium commercial photography, center-safe composition, no readable text,
> no logos, no watermark, 9:16.

### Clip 3 — the confident outcome

> Vertical cinematic shot of the same kind of small-business owner smiling as
> a polished mobile-first business website appears on a laptop and phone. Show
> the emotional outcome—clarity, credibility, and readiness for customers—while
> keeping device screens abstract so text is added later in Adobe Express or
> Premiere. Black, white, and electric-lime accent lighting, subtle camera
> orbit, premium realistic commercial style, center-safe composition, no
> readable text, no logo, no watermark, 9:16.

## Assembly directions

Create a 20–30 second 1080×1920 master in Adobe Express or Premiere:

1. Hook in the first two seconds: **Still putting off your website?**
2. Alternate the presenter with the three B-roll clips.
3. Burn in large captions within the center-safe region.
4. Add only the real FAMtastic logo asset during editing; do not depend on
   generated logo text.
5. Use black, white, and electric lime. Avoid generic Adobe template colors.
6. End on a two-second branded card:
   - **A professional basic website. $199.**
   - **About 55 cents a day for the first year.**
   - **Cost is not one of them. Period.**
   - `famtasticdesigns.com/55-cents-a-day-website`
7. Export H.264 MP4, 1080×1920, with AAC audio.

## Subscription decision

- Prefer **HeyGen** when a polished presenter-led advertisement can be produced
  from one brief with materially less assembly and revision time.
- Prefer **Firefly + Adobe Express/Premiere** when brand control, cinematic
  B-roll, manual editing, and an already-owned Adobe subscription outweigh the
  extra assembly work.
- Keep **Remotion** as the reusable no-presenter baseline and bulk-variation
  engine regardless of which presenter provider wins.
- Do not buy either provider merely because one sample looks impressive. Buy
  when the expected approved-video volume makes the saved production time worth
  more than the subscription.
