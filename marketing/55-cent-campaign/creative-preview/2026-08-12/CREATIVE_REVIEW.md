# 55 cents campaign creative preview

Date: 2026-08-12
State: Creative review only
Publishing authorization: None

## Purpose

Test three distinct visual territories before connecting social accounts or
building the complete 17-day asset library. All final typography is rendered
deterministically so offer terms, punctuation, logo treatment, and crop safety
do not depend on generated image text.

## Concepts

### Ad 01 — The price objection ends here

- Visual: bakery owner seeing business activity on her phone.
- Job: make the entry price feel accessible without presenting the work as
  disposable or cheap.
- Headline: “A professional website. About 55¢ a day.”
- CTA: “See what’s included.”
- Scope guardrail: identifies Web Basics, included first-year basic hosting,
  and the conditional new-domain/existing-domain path.

### Ad 02 — Great work deserves to be found

- Visual: skilled barber inside the shop while potential customers search as
  they pass outside.
- Job: dramatize the discovery and credibility problem rather than leading
  with price.
- Headline: “Great work deserves to be found.”
- CTA: “Get a recommendation.”
- Scope guardrail: does not imply Web Basics fits every business.

### Ad 03 — Your foot in the door

- Visual: owner crossing through a browser-shaped doorway into visibility.
- Job: create the bold campaign-memory device and connect it to a practical
  first step.
- Headline: “Stop waiting. Start showing up.”
- CTA: “Explore Web Basics.”
- Scope guardrail: explicitly says the $199 offer applies when Web Basics is
  the right fit.

## QA status

- Generated text in source photography: none.
- Deterministic FAMtastic lockup, CTA, URL, and campaign ID: present.
- Output: 4:5 portrait, 2160×2700 PNG.
- Brand palette: near-black, white, and electric lime.
- Customer result claims or fabricated statistics: none.
- Public post, paid ad, account connection, or recipient action: none.

The source images and rendered ads are retained together for non-destructive
iteration. The reusable renderer is `scripts/render-campaign-ad.swift`.
