## 1. Concept: "The Rented Land Trap" (Pain Point / Comparison)

* **File**: `01-rented-land-vs-owned-hub.jpg`
* **Aspect Ratio**: 1:1 (Square - Feed / Carousel Slide 1)
* **Target Audience**: Service providers, independent professionals, beauty/barbers, contractors relying on Instagram DMs, Linktree, and 3rd-party platforms with heavy cutouts.
* **Core Value Prop**: Stop losing 20% in fees and owning zero data. Own your domain, booking flow, and client portal.
* **Prompt Used**:
```text
A high-impact split advertising visual for a modern web design agency campaign titled 'Stop Building on Rented Land'. On the left side: chaotic, fragmented mobile UI with generic link-in-bio buttons, crowded social media DMs, platform fee deductions, and dull flat colors. On the right side: a gorgeous, ultra-premium custom branded web application and client portal displayed on a sleek glass smartphone with dark obsidian background, glowing electric lime and neon chartreuse accents, smooth glassmorphism cards, verified domain badge, and rising booking revenue charts. Dramatic studio lighting, cinematic contrast, 8k resolution, modern commercial graphic design.
```
