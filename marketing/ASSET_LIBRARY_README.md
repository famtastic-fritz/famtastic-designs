# FAMtastic Marketing Asset Library

This library contains reusable creative and production evidence for FAMtastic Designs.

## Contents

- 55-cents-a-day campaign images for Facebook, Instagram, Stories/Reels, TikTok, and video scenes.
- Photoreal campaign source images and creative-review proofs.
- Rendered campaign videos and QA frames.
- FAMtastic Guide cartoon avatar artwork.
- Matched narration and the completed HeyGen comparison video.
- Adobe-pipeline creative proofs, emails, tracking evidence, and campaign specifications.
- Campaign playbooks, automation documentation, and the canonical visual marketing-system map.

## Operating Rule

Source assets, approved outputs, prompts, campaign manifests, tracking details, and QA evidence remain linked. Do not publish an asset merely because it exists in this library; confirm its approval state and destination first.

## Canonical Workflow

`THOUGHT → PRODUCT → CAMPAIGN → IMAGES + ASSETS → AVATAR VIDEO → QA + APPROVAL → PUBLISH → CUSTOMER → ANALYTICS → LEARNING`
