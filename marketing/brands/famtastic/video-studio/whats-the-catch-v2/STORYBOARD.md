# What's the catch? — authored storyboard

## Video direction

A transparent offer becomes a foundation. Alternate dark brand address with warm
paper explainers; change scale, density and composition at each argument turn.
Exact source logo and brand fonts throughout. Every reveal follows its real
narration cue. Objects build, connect or transform; they do not float endlessly.
“Why?” and the final “You grow. We grow.” have deliberate still reads. No stock
actor, generic charts, fabricated outcomes, hidden terms or remote media fetches.

Times are derived mechanically from zero-based nonempty lines of user-script.txt
and saved in the staged project's scene-timing.json. Each frame below names its
line range; this avoids guessed timestamps before narration is synthesized.

| Frame | Voice lines | Visual sequence and intention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 — The question | 0–1 | Dark editorial 60/40. A tangible $199 starter ticket settles at right as the first sentence is spoken; “What's the catch?” lands at left on the question. A price is the hook, not a fabricated statistic. |
| 02 — Nothing hidden | 2 | Warm paper, readable offer document at right. “No hidden fees.” then “No surprise add-ons.” at left. Included foundation, first-year hosting/domain terms and separate renewal treatment remain visible. |
| 03 — The philosophy | 3–4 | Dark full-width FAMtastic word treatment with the actual logo above; shift from the price to “Belief comes first.” A foundation line grows underneath the sentence as philosophy is named. |
| 04 — We believe in you | 5–9 | Paper centered address with green handwritten “you.” The words VISION, HUSTLE, GRIND arrive one by one; a growing business foundation unifies them. |
| 05 — Vision first | 10 | Dark split. Three solid blocks form a base under YOUR VISION, illustrating support at the beginning. “Your vision. First.” carries the frame. |
| 06 — Build the business | 11 | Warm paper triptych. A labelled website illustration, working plan and tool/strategy sheet reveal on their spoken phrases. Clear tangible objects, no fake submitted forms. |
| 07 — Why? | 12–13 | Obsidian monumental WHY? holds, then makes room for the exact confidence statement. Typographic emphasis and a drawn rule; no unverifiable badge or number. |
| 08 — Prove the value | 14 | Paper asymmetric composition. A path climbs tangible steps toward NEXT LEVEL; “Let us prove our value.” replaces the first headline when that phrase is spoken. No chart axes or claimed outcomes. |
| 09 — When you're ready | 15 | Green/obsidian, broad foundation and increasing building levels. “Build. Grow. Evolve.” reveals across the sentence; WHEN YOU'RE READY keeps the promise conditional. |
| 10 — The first believer | 16 | Paper, return to the small starting block beneath the larger structure. “Remember who believed in your vision.” and “From the beginning.” make the callback explicit. |
| 11 — Shared growth | 17–20 | Dark split YO​U/FAMtastic structures grow together, then “You grow. We grow.” settles. The exact logo and clean famtasticdesigns.com destination hold through the final spoken URL and two seconds afterward. |

Narration is synthesized locally, not impersonating Fritz. Captions reflect the
spoken script; public playback also offers a VTT track and complete transcript.
