# What's the catch? — film direction

The concrete object is a transparent starter offer that becomes a business
foundation. This is a fresh motion-graphics film, not a reskin of the presenter
movie. No footage, music or actor performance from that movie is used.

Use the repository's `paper` palette (#f4f1ea / #1f6f4a) for clarity, proposals,
plans and an honest readable offer; use the canonical obsidian/lime world for
FAMtastic's personal voice. Warm gold emphasizes belief and continuity. This
palette shift follows the argument instead of swapping colors arbitrarily.

1920×1080, 30 fps; Metropolis display/body with short Kaushan signature accents.
Titles 90–126 px, object labels 28–36 px, captions 40 px. Content safe area
100–1820 horizontally and 190–870 vertically. Captions have a separate 900–994
band; exact creator-credit markup holds below that. Logo/header persists above
chapter objects. No decorative object crosses text or caption bands.

Motion grammar: purposeful object reveals on the spoken cue; restrained power3
settles; tall objects build from their foundation; plan cards turn into a three-
part working system. Deliberate held beats for “Why?” and the closing phrase.
No looped floating, random particles, fake customer figures or revenue charts.
All SVGs are authored explanatory objects, not photo substitutes or redrawn logos.

The film keeps the owner's supplied narration; the public page and on-screen
scope card disclose the real offer, separate hosting authorization after year
one, and domain renewal at disclosed registrar price. Additional services remain
optional. “Invest” describes the business philosophy, not financing or an equity
investment.

Production decisions and constraints are in BRIEF.md. Cue timing comes from the
actual local narration; no scene is timed from an assumed speaking speed.
