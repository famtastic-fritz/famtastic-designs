## What this proves

- One capability can move through acquisition, education, and retention use cases.
- Each scenario has a distinct audience, promise, visual metaphor, CTA, and UTM.
- The FAMtastic black/electric-lime identity remains recognizable across offers.
- Original campaign visuals and publishable draft copy can be produced together.
