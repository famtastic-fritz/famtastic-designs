# The Sign That Isn't There — 44s master

Six beats, one per section of `video-scripts/EP1_FULL_SCRIPT.md`. The script's
own timings (0–6, 6–16, 16–28, 28–42, 42–52, 52–58) are **narration** timings for
a 58s voiced cut. These films carry no voiceover, so those beats are re-timed to
reading speed; holding a silent frame for ten seconds is dead air, not pacing.
The re-timing is the only place this cut departs from the script — the argument,
its order, and its wording are the script's.

## The light shaft

One `4px` amber hairline is the film's whole accent budget. It enters from the
left, holds, and leaves, and it sits one step lower in the frame on each beat:

| beat | shaft y | on |
|---|---|---|
| hook | 254 | under the eyebrow |
| absence | 254 | under the eyebrow |
| reframe | 254 | under the eyebrow |
| fix | 1244 | across the blank card |
| offer | 1214 | the price marker |
| close | 1330 | across the empty bracket |

Beat 6 returns to beat 1's photograph with the shaft now low on the bracket and a
name set where a sign would hang. That return is the payoff.
