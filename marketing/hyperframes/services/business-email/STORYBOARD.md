# Two Different Jobs

Five beats. **Every scene boundary sits in a measured gap between sentences of
the approved take-b narration**, not at an estimated time. The word timings
came from `hyperframes transcribe` (87 words, whisper) run against the take;
the gaps used are 1.66-1.92, 9.60-9.79, 18.12-18.31, 23.20-23.39 and
26.71-26.99 s. No cut lands mid-sentence.

| beat | scene | start | dur | composition | narration it carries |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| question | 01-question | 0.00 | 9.70 | presenter on paper | the question, the answer, and why it is said plainly |
| basics | 02-basics | 9.70 | 8.52 | split band on plate | what Web Basics is |

## Frame 1 - question

- id: `01-question`, motion: `waterfall-entry` + a panel wipe
- The keyed presenter stands on the paper ground at bottom-right. Eyebrow
  `A QUESTION WE GET A LOT`; `DOES THE $199 WEBSITE / COME WITH BUSINESS EMAIL?`
  cascades at 1.90, under the narration's own question (1.92-5.69).
- **At 5.50 an ink-coloured block wipes open and at 5.72 the answer lands in
  it: `IT DOES NOT.`** — reversed out in paper colour. It is the only inverted
  block in the film. The narration says the words at 5.69; the block is already
  there when they arrive.
- Support line at 6.72: *"We would rather say that plainly than let you find
  out later."* (narration 6.70-9.60).
- She leaves at 9.36. The film hands off from a person to a document.

## Frame 2 - basics

- id: `02-basics`, motion: `waterfall-entry` + `viewport-change` (1.000 -> 1.040)
- `split`: a full-width band with a 50px diagonal on its lower edge, cut across
  a full-bleed plate of a blank card in a brass holder. The photograph stays
  visible above and below it.
- Eyebrow `WEB BASICS BUNDLE - $199`, headline `THREE THINGS.`, then one row per
  clause of the narration: `One landing-page website` (10.38), `One year of
  managed hosting` (12.04), `Your domain, new or already yours` (13.82).

## Frame 3 - renewal

- id: `03-renewal`, motion: `waterfall-entry` + `viewport-change` (1.040 -> 1.065)
- The band grows 740 -> 820 px; the photograph recedes.
- Eyebrow `AFTER THE FIRST YEAR`, headline `WHAT RENEWS.`, rows
  `Hosting $9.99 a month` (narration 19.30-21.17) and `Your domain renews
  separately` (21.36-23.20).

## Frame 4 - email

- id: `04-email`, motion: `spring-pop-entrance`
- The photograph stops being a ground and becomes an object: a 912 x 608 card
  with a real cast shadow, carrying the campaign anchor — two blank business
  cards on a workbench, the same business printed twice. The anchor is natively
  3:2 and the card is 3:2, so nothing is cropped and both cards stay in frame.
- Eyebrow `BUSINESS EMAIL SETUP`, `$99` pops at 0.44 with `ONE TIME`,
  *"A separate setup, on the domain you already own."*, and
  *"You keep your own mailbox provider. We never resell mailboxes."*

## Frame 5 - close

- id: `05-close`, motion: `waterfall-entry`
- **The card does not move across the cut** — same position, same scale. Only
  the words under it change.
- `TWO DIFFERENT / JOBS.` at 0.14 (narration 26.99-28.16), then both products
  on one frame: `Web Basics Bundle $199 one time` and `Business Email Setup $99
  one time`, so the last image cannot be read as a bundle. Then the accent
  marker, `famtasticdesigns.com/packages` (curled, HTTP 200), and *"Ask us
  which one you need first."* (narration 28.16-29.50).
- The film then holds two seconds on a finished frame rather than cutting on
  the last syllable.
