# BRIEF — "Two Different Jobs"

**Deliverable.** One 31.5 s, 1080 x 1920, 30 fps film for `FAM-BUSINESS-EMAIL`,
authored in HyperFrames and rendered locally.

**Message.** Business email is not part of the $199 website bundle. It is its
own product at $99, one time. Two different jobs.

**Why this film exists.** `marketing/campaigns/cost-is-not-the-reason/posting-schedule.json`
carries drops that say the $199 bundle includes "a branded business email
address". `backend/config/famtastic-products.json` says otherwise:
`FAM-BUSINESS-EMAIL` is a separate SKU at $99. The claim is under owner review.
This film is the correction, and the correction is the whole piece rather than
a footnote on it.

**The narration was already approved and already says it.**
`marketing/creative/heygen/renders/take-b-business-email-scope.mp4` is an
existing HeyGen take whose script states the separateness plainly, discloses
the $9.99/month renewal and the separate domain renewal, and ends "Two
different jobs. Ask us which one you need first." Nothing had to be written; it
had to be cut to.

**Audience.** Someone who has bought, or is about to buy, the $199 bundle and
would otherwise find out later.

**Constraints accepted up front.**
- Every figure from `backend/config/famtastic-products.json` and the approved
  take-b script. No statistic, no percentage, no named competitor.
- Graded to `marketing/creative/heygen/reference-tokens.json`.
- Reuse existing assets only. No generation of any kind.
- The film must be unmisreadable: the close puts both products and both prices
  on the same frame.

**Aspect.** Portrait only.

**Sound.** The take-b narration, verbatim and untrimmed, for 29.48 s of a
31.5 s film.
