# BRIEF — "Found, Then Kept"

**Deliverable.** One 28.0 s, 1080 x 1920, 30 fps film covering `FAM-LOCAL-SEO`
and `FAM-MAINTENANCE`, authored in HyperFrames and rendered locally.

**Message.** A shop can be open and still have nothing for search to find.
Local SEO Setup is what makes it readable; Website Maintenance is what keeps it
that way. Two separate products, each with its own price, and neither is in the
$199 bundle.

**Why the two products share one film.** They are the two halves of the same
job — being found, and staying found — and they are the two most likely things
for a $199 customer to assume are already included. Putting them in one film
with the separateness stated on screen is safer than two films that each leave
the other unmentioned.

**Audience.** A business that has just launched, or is about to, and is
wondering what happens next.

**Constraints accepted up front.**
- Every figure from `backend/config/famtastic-products.json`; the deliverable
  lines condensed from the campaigns' own landing and email copy.
- **No ranking promise, anywhere.** `BRAND.md` forbids it; the film says so on
  screen rather than merely avoiding it.
- Graded to `marketing/creative/heygen/reference-tokens.json`.
- Reuse existing assets only. No generation of any kind.

**Aspect.** Portrait only.

**Sound.** None. The repo holds no approved narration for either product and no
licensed music, and this task authorised no provider spend. The pacing is set
for reading. Recorded as a limitation in the lane README, not hidden.
