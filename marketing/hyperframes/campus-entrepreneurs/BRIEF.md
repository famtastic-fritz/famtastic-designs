## Intent

A campaign film for `marketing/campaigns/campus-entrepreneurs/`, aimed at
college students running a side hustle between classes — photography, tutoring,
nails and beauty on campus, sneaker resale, mobile detailing, fitness coaching,
digital services.

The argument is the one the campaign's own posting package makes: the work is
already a business, and the only thing missing is an address the student owns.
Warm, confident, not hypey — the audience is a working founder, not a spectator.

## Length is set by the bed

27.5s, because the music bed comes from the campaign's own attached video
(27.6s) and the film is cut to end inside it with a fade rather than a hard stop.

## Assets — reused, nothing generated

- `marketing/campaigns/campus-entrepreneurs/videos/01-campus-dorm-entrepreneur-9x16.mp4`
  — the campaign's own attached video. Native 1080×1920 @ 30fps, so it is the
  composition's exact size. Supplies BOTH the opening picture (graded, silent)
  and the music bed (trimmed, level-trimmed, faded).
- `marketing/campaigns/campus-entrepreneurs/images/01-campus-quad-entrepreneur-1x1.jpg`
  — campus quad at golden hour, as a card.
- `marketing/creative/plates/platform-dependency/pd-p-vertical-9x16.jpg` and
  `pd-b2-vertical-9x16.jpg` — two photographs of blank surfaces already on disk,
  each carrying its own small olive incident.

All grading is local ffmpeg. No provider call, no image generation, $0.

`LIVE_POSTING_PACKAGE_CAMPUS.md` lists deliverables that `FAM-FOOT-199` does not
