# Brief — I've Managed Fine

**workflow**: general-video
**flow**: autonomous

## What this is

Six 9:16 films, 20-24 seconds each, one per objection, for FAMtastic Designs'
$199 Web Basics package (`FAM-FOOT-199`).

## Who it is for

An established business owner, usually older, who has run a good business for
years with no website and genuinely does not believe they need one. Real
customers, real revenue, no web presence. Expert in their trade, beginner on
anything web. Many have been burned once by a "computer guy".

They are not lazy and not stupid. They are pattern-matching on decades of
evidence that word of mouth worked — because it did.

## The six objections, in their own words

1. "I've managed fine for thirty years." — true, and the customers who found you did it differently each decade.
2. "My customers know where I am." — the ones you have, yes. The question is the ones who moved in last year.
3. "I'm not technical." — you will not touch it. Neither did anyone else who has one.
4. "I tried before and got burned." — name the real pattern: paid up front, no proof, then silence.
5. "It's too expensive." — 55 cents a day, one page, one year of hosting, a domain that is yours.
6. "I'm too close to retiring." — a buyer buys what transfers, and a domain transfers.

## Hard rules

- **Never mock the objection.** Never imply they are behind. The words
  "still", "finally" and "even you" appear nowhere — in copy, narration, or
  on-screen type. Respect is the whole tone.
- **Each film stands alone.** The owner sends these one-to-one, so no film may
  depend on having seen another, and every one ends with the price and the
  address.
- **Every film carries audio.** A silent film is not acceptable for this
  campaign. Narration is local Voicebox / kokoro (`af_heart`, the house
  "FAMtastic Narrator" profile) — free, no API key, no metered credits.
- **No statistic, no percentage, no named competitor, no ranking promise, no
  delivery-time promise.** Argue the mechanism. See `frame.md` → "Claims
  allowed on screen".
- **Graded to the anchor**, `marketing/creative/heygen/reference-tokens.json`:
  mean luminance 150-175, olive `#7FB449` at 1-2 % of frame.
