# Fifty-Five Cents

Five beats over **one photograph**. A quote sheet is laid on it and grows
upward as more is written on it; the camera runs one unbroken push
(1.000 -> 1.022 -> 1.050 -> 1.078 -> 1.098) across beats 1-4, so every cut
lands on the same frame and the four beats read as a single held shot. The
close cuts to a second photograph.

| beat | scene | start | dur | plate | sheet top | carries |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| objection | 01-objection | 0.00 | 4.60 | owner | 1160 | the claim |
| arithmetic | 02-arithmetic | 4.60 | 6.00 | owner | 740 | the sum |
| scope | 03-scope | 10.60 | 7.00 | owner | 620 | what $199 is |
| renewal | 04-renewal | 17.60 | 5.80 | owner | 560 | what renews, what is separate |
| close | 05-close | 23.40 | 4.60 | counter | 1100 | reprise + address |

The sheet grows 540 -> 960 -> 1080 -> 1140 px and its bottom edge never moves
from y 1700. That progression is the argument: the document fills in.

## Frame 1 - objection

- id: `01-objection`, motion: `waterfall-entry` + `viewport-change`
- Eyebrow `THE OBJECTION`. `COST IS NOT / THE REASON.` cascades at 1.05.
- The plate is a real business already trading: an owner at her counter. The
  objection belongs to a person, so the film opens on one.
- Narration: *"Of all the reasons you do not have a professional website yet,
  cost is not one of them."* (0.06-4.40)

## Frame 2 - arithmetic

- id: `02-arithmetic`, motion: `spring-pop-entrance` (figure, marker) +
  `waterfall-entry` (rows)
- `55c` pops at 0.50 with its accent marker, `A DAY` beside it. Then the proof
  as three ledger rows: `First year, one time $199.00` / `Days in the year 365`
  / `Cost per day $0.545`, and the footnote *"$0.545 rounds to 55c. That is the
  whole sum - you can do it yourself."*
- Narration: *"At 55 cents a day, $199 for your entire first year,"*
  (4.40-10.16). The voice hands off to the page mid-clause and the page
  finishes the sentence.

## Frame 3 - scope

- id: `03-scope`, motion: `waterfall-entry`
- Eyebrow `WHAT THE $199 IS`. `ONE PAGE, / ONE YEAR, / ONE ADDRESS.` then the
  three items verbatim in substance from the SKU summary, and *"That is the
  whole bundle. Nothing else is included in it."*

## Frame 4 - renewal

- id: `04-renewal`, motion: `waterfall-entry`
- Eyebrow `AFTER THE FIRST YEAR`. `THE PART / MOST PRICES / LEAVE OFF.`
- Two rows of what renews (`Hosting, from year two $9.99 / month`;
  `Your domain renews separately`), then a hairline, then the label
  `SEPARATE PRODUCTS` **before** the two rows it governs, so nothing above it
  can be read as including them: `Business email $99, one time` and
  `Website maintenance $49.99 / month`. Footnote: *"Neither one is included in
  the $199."*
- This beat is why the film exists.

## Frame 5 - close

- id: `05-close`, motion: `waterfall-entry` + `spring-pop-entrance`
- The film cuts to a workbench counter with a blank card on it. `COST IS NOT /
  THE REASON.` reprises, then the accent marker and
  `famtasticdesigns.com/packages` (curled, HTTP 200 on apex and www), and
  *"Read the whole scope before you buy anything."*
