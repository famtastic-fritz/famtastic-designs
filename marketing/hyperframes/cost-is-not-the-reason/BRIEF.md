# BRIEF — "Fifty-Five Cents"

**Deliverable.** One 28.0 s, 1080 x 1920, 30 fps campaign film for the live
`cost-is-not-the-reason` campaign, authored in HyperFrames and rendered locally.

**Message.** Cost is not the reason you do not have a website yet. Here is the
arithmetic, here is exactly what the money buys, and here is the part most
price films leave off — what renews, and what was never included.

**Why this film, and not another 55-cent ad.** The campaign already has a
15-second 55-cent commercial. What it does not have anywhere is a piece that
survives being checked: the division written out, the renewal disclosed, and
the two products that are NOT in the bundle named with their own prices. That
is the whole differentiator, and it is also `BRAND.md`'s standing requirement
("Disclose renewal terms whenever first-year pricing is mentioned"), which the
existing campaign assets do not meet.

**Audience.** A business already doing real business with no website — the
primary audience in `BRAND.md`. Expert in their trade, beginner here, and
reasonably suspicious of a low price.

**Constraints accepted up front.**
- Every figure from `backend/config/famtastic-products.json` and the live
  package page. No statistic, no percentage, no named competitor, no delivery
  promise, no ranking promise.
- Graded to `marketing/creative/heygen/reference-tokens.json`: 150-175 mean
  luminance, `#7FB449` accent at 1-2% of frame, `#33272E` shadow floor.
- Reuse existing assets only. No image, video, voice or model generation.
- **Nothing may state or imply that business email is in the $199 bundle.**

**Aspect.** Portrait only. HyperFrames bakes aspect into the composition; see
README.md.

**Sound.** Two sentences of narration reused from the campaign's own commercial
and trimmed before its one unsupported claim. Silent from 10.45 s.
