# Creative Director & Message Architecture Standard (Non-Visual Edition)

This skill translates OpenAI's image prompting cookbook into a **pure logic, copywriting, campaign architecture, and script engineering framework**. 

The same principles that produce award-winning images—4-part structural grammar, explicit invariant constraints, sequential beat pacing, and verbatim execution—produce high-converting marketing copy and campaign narratives.

---

## 1. The 4-Part Structural Copy Grammar

Just as visual prompts fail when unstructured, marketing copy fails when written as runaway text. Every high-converting message must follow this 4-part architectural order:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                          4-PART MESSAGE GRAMMAR HIERARCHY                              │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. CONTEXT & STATE       │ The prospect's current friction, environment, & emotional state│
│ 2. HERO VEHICLE & ACTION │ The transformative solution (tools, knowledge, access)     │
│ 3. PROOF & PROPOSITION   │ Tangible numbers, unit economics, demonstration of craft    │
│ 4. INVARIANTS & OFFER    │ Exact risk-reversal terms, call to action, what we promise  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. The 7 Non-Visual Creative Blueprints

### Blueprint 1: The Direct-Response Hook & Inversion ("Rented vs Owned")
* **Principle**: Mirror the "Photorealism vs CGI" contrast—juxtapose the gritty ordinary against the manifested extraordinary.
* **Architecture**:
  1. **Pattern Interrupt (0–3s)**: Call out the silent revenue leak (e.g., *"If you run your business on Instagram DMs and @gmail, you're paying a 30% tax on your own hard work."*)
  2. **The Inversion**: Reframe the problem not as a lack of effort, but as building on rented land.
  3. **The Proof Proposition**: *"We give you your own custom domain, enterprise email, and AI growth tools for 55¢ a day."*
  4. **The Safe Action**: 3 live interactive working concepts delivered in 48 hours before committing.

### Blueprint 2: Sequential Narrative Pacing (The Story-to-Script Formula)
Based on Section 4.7 (Story-to-Comic Strip), every video script or case study must follow a 4-beat sequential progression:
* **Beat 1 (The Status Quo Friction)**: The master craftsman trapped in administrative chaos.
* **Beat 2 (The Catalyst / Shift in Posture)**: Refusing to stay an ordinary hustle on rented land.
* **Beat 3 (The Manifested Engine)**: The high-speed digital hub taking direct bookings with 0% platform cuts.
* **Beat 4 (The Sovereign Climax)**: Business thriving on owned infrastructure.

### Blueprint 3: Surgical Copy Editing ("Change ONLY X, Preserve Y")
Based on Section 5.1 & 5.7 (Style Transfer & Surgical Editing), when revising marketing copy or pitch scripts:
* **State the exact delta**: *"Change ONLY the value proposition in Section 2 to emphasize local SEO authority."*
* **Lock the invariants**: *"Preserve the exact tone, paragraph rhythm, founder voice, and 55¢ pricing terms unchanged."*

### Blueprint 4: Pitch Deck Slide Architecture (Artifact Specification)
Based on Section 4.10, write pitch deck content as structured artifact specs:
* **Header / Title**: One declarative assertion (not a generic label like "Market Overview").
* **Core Metric Callout**: Clear mathematical TAM/SAM/SOM or unit economics.
* **Supporting Data**: 3 bulleted data points with source citations.
* **Footer Anchor**: The strategic takeaway.

### Blueprint 5: Multi-Audience Localization & Translation Spec
Based on Section 4.2 (Translation in Images), when adapting a campaign across different vertical niches (Beauty vs Auto vs Retail):
* **Preserve the Structural Skeleton**: Hook $\rightarrow$ Problem $\rightarrow$ FAMtastic Backing $\rightarrow$ 55¢ Offer $\rightarrow$ 48h Proof.
* **Surgically Swap the Niche Lexicon**:
  * *Beauty*: Replace "parts/labor" with "shears/balayage", replace "shop management" with "Booksy fee escape".
  * *Auto*: Replace "appointments" with "repair orders/estimates", replace "booking app" with "fleet invoices".

### Blueprint 6: Irresistible Risk-Reversal Guarantee ("The 55¢ Covenant")
* **Formula**: Upfront demonstration + Zero-risk entry + High-ticket enterprise firepower.
* **The Script**:
  > *"We believe in your business idea. Before you spend thousands on an agency, we give you 1 year of cloud hosting, custom domain, business email, and AI-backed growth data for 55 cents a day ($199/yr). In 48 hours, we deliver 3 live working concepts. If you don't love the results, you're out 55 cents and we're out a promise made. Let's prove the process works."*

### Blueprint 7: The Brand Lexicon & Tone Anchor
* **Voice**: Confident, grounded, authoritative, craft-focused.
* **Avoid**: Generic buzzwords (*"synergy"*, *"cutting-edge"*, *"leverage"*).
* **Emphasize**: Demonstrable outcomes (*"owned digital hub"*, *"0% take rates"*, *"instant proof in 48 hours"*).
