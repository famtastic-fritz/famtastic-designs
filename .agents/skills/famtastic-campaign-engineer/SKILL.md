## 1. Research & Angle Deconstruction

When given any campaign idea (e.g. *"55 cents a day / we believe in your business"*, *"Estée Lauder-scale commerce for local artisans"*, *"Campus dorm entrepreneur"*):

1. **The Fearless Inversion (F)**:
   * How does this offer boldly deviate from the industry standard? (e.g. traditional agencies demand $5,000 upfront; FAMtastic invests upfront with a $199 full-year all-inclusive bundle).
2. **The Mastery Demonstration (A)**:
   * What is the immediate tangible proof? (e.g. 3 live interactive design directions delivered in 48 hours: Safe, Wild, OMG).
3. **The Manifestation Proof (M)**:
   * How does this turn an ordinary local operator into an extraordinary, modern market leader? (e.g. moving from `@gmail`/DM chaos to custom domain, business email, real-time analytics, and AI-backed growth data).

## 2. Modular Design Prompt Cookbook

### The Prompt Architecture Formula:
```text
[SCENE_TYPE] + [SUBJECT_ACTION] + [ENVIRONMENT_DETAILS] + [LIGHTING_ATMOSPHERE] + [CAMERA_OPTICS] + [COLOR_PALETTE] + [NEGATIVE_CONSTRAINTS]
```

### Modular Delta Slots:
* `[SUBJECT]`: Master stylist, auto mechanic, mobile detailer, dorm entrepreneur, boutique baker.
* `[ENVIRONMENT]`: High-end modern salon, industrial garage with neon backlights, college dorm tech lab.
* `[LIGHTING]`: Editorial rim lighting, high-contrast chiaroscuro, natural golden hour sunlight.
* `[COLOR_PALETTE]`: Campaign-specific tones (e.g. Obsidian `#080808` + Chartreuse `#7CFC00` for core brand; Cobalt `#0052FF` + Copper `#D97706` for auto repair; Warm Terracotta `#C2410C` + Gold `#F59E0B` for campus hustle).

### The "Photo-Only" Rule:
* **CRITICAL**: Never ask image models to render text, prices (`$199`), logos, or UI buttons.
* **Always prompt for clean commercial photography with generous negative space on one third of the frame.**
* Layer crisp typography natively in HTML/CSS, Photoshop JSX export, or Remotion video layers.

## 4. Multi-Channel Distribution Package Template

Every campaign engineered through this skill must output:
1. **Facebook Conversion Post**: Value proposition, math breakdown, and direct checkout link.
2. **Twitter/X 3-Tweet Thread**: Contrarian hook + video clip + math proof.
3. **Instagram / TikTok Script**: 30-second short-form script with Safe-Zone margins (top 240px, bottom 380px padding).
4. **YouTube Long-Form Explainer (16:9)**: 3-5 minute comprehensive breakdown with timestamps and pinned comment.
5. **Campaign Ledger**: JSON record tracking prompts, hashes, run times, and UTM parameters.
