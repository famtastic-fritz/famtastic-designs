**Status:** project-shared working context

**Authority:** derived from the repository sources linked below; it is not a

## What FAMtastic Designs is

FAMtastic Designs engineers connected digital business systems for small and
growing businesses. A website is often the entry point; the customer outcome is
getting found, getting customers, getting paid, serving customers, and growing
through connected intake, operations, communication, automation, and
measurement.

## Who it is for

- Owners and operators who need a credible digital presence and a practical way
  to turn attention into a managed customer relationship.
- Local services, events, community-led organizations, and growing businesses
  that need a site plus an operating path behind it.
- Customers who may need a bounded package, a recommendation, or a scoped
  custom conversation. Do not force every lead into a starter website offer.

## How FAMtastic should sound

Clear, direct, confident, practical, and human. Teach first, make the next
action useful, and do not substitute generic agency language for specific proof.

## Marketing and conversion rules

1. Start with the relevant customer job and evidence-supported outcome, not a
   list of tools or a generic “contact us” CTA.
2. Separate demand creation (teach, demonstrate, prove) from demand capture
   (assessment, recommendation, configured purchase, or scoped request).
3. Treat every fact, performance claim, testimonial, price, offer inclusion,
   availability promise, and customer result as untrusted until traced to the
   linked source of truth.
4. Every campaign, page, asset, and social item needs a stable identifier,
   evidence/claim basis, CTA, destination, approval state, and measurement
   plan. Build DNA is mandatory for creative proofs and media.
5. A draft, local test, or provider response is not permission to publish,
   email, spend, discount, promise, or charge.

## Specialist routing

Load only the narrowest relevant skill after reading this file. Use `cro` for
marketing-page or form conversion, `signup`/`onboarding`/`popups` for the
customer journey, `revops` for lead-to-service handoff, `offers` for offer
design, `ad-creative`/`social` for approved campaign drafts,
`marketing-ideas`/`marketing-loops` for strategy, `ai-seo`/`schema`/`site-architecture`
for discovery, `analytics`/`ab-testing` for measurement, and
`sales-enablement` for approved sales collateral.

These specialist skills are supporting methods. They cannot override this
context or create external effects without the FAMtastic approval gates.
