# Tighten Up Your Locs admin design contract

Created before independent-admin implementation, September14,2026. This app serves Shay's business, not the FAMtastic customer portal. Preserve Ruby Signal: cream #efe6d4, cocoa #311f1a, ruby #8f1831, gold #d6ae62. No agency lime portal theme. Follow public pilot design.md for shared type and brand continuity.

Show business name, current task and one primary next action. Admin navigation: Requests, Calendar, Openings. Keep real empty/error states; no demo clients, earnings or appointments. The calendar shows authoritative saved appointments and selected-day detail. Requests are not reservations; confirmation creates the reservation before success appears.

Independent sign-in and password setup/reset, accessible labels, errors next to forms, no public registration, no agency login links. Host-local assets; all controls >=44px, focus visible, 390/768/1280 no overflow. Do not put private contact details or owner pages into analytics. Footer agency credit may link out, but it is not required for app operation.
